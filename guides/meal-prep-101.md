# How to Plan a Week of Mediterranean Meals

Meal prep isn't about cooking 21 meals on Sunday. It's about **doing 60% of the work on Sunday** so the rest of the week takes 15 minutes per meal.

This guide walks you through exactly what to prep, how to store it, and how to assemble fresh meals in under 15 minutes during the week.

## The Sunday 1-Hour Method

You don't need a 4-hour meal prep marathon. Here's the realistic 1-hour Sunday plan:

### Minute 0-15: Protein + Grains
- **Bake 4 chicken breasts** at 200°C (25 min in oven while you prep everything else)
- **Cook 1 cup couscous** (5 min, let cool, store)
- **Cook 1 cup brown rice** (25 min)

### Minute 15-30: Beans + Vegetables
- **Open 2 cans chickpeas** + 1 can white beans (rinse, drain, store in fridge)
- **Roast a tray of vegetables** at 220°C: zucchini, bell peppers, cherry tomatoes (20 min in oven)

### Minute 30-45: Sauces + Dressings
- **Make a batch of tahini-lemon dressing**: ½ cup tahini + lemon juice + garlic + water (5 min)
- **Make a batch of tomato sauce**: canned tomatoes + garlic + olive oil + herbs (10 min)

### Minute 45-60: Assembly + Storage
- **Portion out 3 chicken breasts** for the week (keep 1 for tonight, fridge the rest)
- **Store beans, grains, veg in glass containers**
- **Label everything** with day-of-week

**Total time**: 60 minutes
**Total dishes**: 1 sheet pan + 1 pot + 1 baking tray

## The Storage System

| Food | Fridge (cooked) | Freezer |
|------|----------------|---------|
| Chicken breasts | 4 days | 3 months |
| Cooked grains (rice, couscous) | 5 days | 2 months |
| Roasted vegetables | 4 days | 3 months |
| Cooked beans | 5 days | 3 months |
| Sauces + dressings | 1 week | 3 months |
| Hard-boiled eggs | 1 week | 2 months |
| Hummus | 1 week | 3 months |

**Pro tip**: Portion into single-serving containers Sunday night so weeknight assembly is truly 15 minutes.

## The 15-Minute Weeknight Assembly

With your Sunday prep done, here's how to make a fresh meal on a Tuesday in 15 minutes:

### Tuesday Night Example: Mediterranean Couscous Bowl
- **Reheat 1 cup cooked couscous** (3 min microwave)
- **Add ½ cup chickpeas** (cold — they don't need heating)
- **Top with ¼ cup roasted veg** (cold — they're delicious at room temp)
- **Drizzle with tahini dressing** (30 sec)
- **Side**: 4 slices cucumber + 1 hard-boiled egg (already prepped)
- **Total time**: 5 minutes

Macros: 485 kcal · 22g protein

### Wednesday Night: Quick Salmon
- **Pan-sear 1 salmon fillet** in olive oil (4 min per side)
- **Reheat ½ cup orzo** from freezer (3 min)
- **Side**: leftover roasted veg + lemon wedge
- **Total time**: 12 minutes

Macros: 420 kcal · 28g protein

## Common Meal Prep Mistakes

❌ **Mistake #1: Prepping too much variety**. Stick to 3-4 proteins, 2-3 grains. Repetition is OK and saves time.

❌ **Mistake #2: Not labeling**. Sunday-night portioning into 5 containers saves the "what was this?" mystery every weeknight.

❌ **Mistake #3: Cooking on a tired Sunday**. Sunday is for rest + light prep. Do heavy cooking on Saturday if needed.

❌ **Mistake #4: Skimping on flavor**. Mediterranean cooking = olive oil + lemon + herbs. **Bland prep = bland week**. Don't shortcut on flavor.
