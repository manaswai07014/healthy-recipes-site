# 30-Minute Weeknight Mediterranean Dinners

Weeknights are hard. You're tired, you're hungry, and you have 30 minutes before someone in your house starts eating crackers for dinner.

These 10 recipes solve that problem. **Every one takes 30 minutes or less**, delivers 400-600 calories with at least 15g protein, and uses ingredients you can find at any Western supermarket.

## The 30-Minute Rule

If a recipe takes longer than 30 minutes on a Tuesday, it doesn't belong in this list. We've filtered our recipes for:

- ✅ **Prep time**: Under 10 minutes
- ✅ **Cook time**: Under 25 minutes
- ✅ **One-pan or one-pot** (less cleanup = faster weeknights)
- ✅ **Pantry-friendly ingredients** (no specialty stores)
- ✅ **Family-friendly** (kids will eat them)

## The 5-Minute Pantry Strategy

The real secret to 30-minute dinners: **keep a stocked pantry**.

Your weeknight pantry needs:
- Canned tomatoes, chickpeas, white beans, tuna
- Whole-wheat pasta, couscous, brown rice
- Olive oil, lemons, garlic
- Capers, olives, dried herbs
- Frozen shrimp, frozen vegetables

With these, you can improvise 80% of weeknight dinners.

## Batch Cooking (Sunday 1 Hour)

Spend 1 hour on Sunday doing:
- **Chop** 1 onion, 1 bell pepper, 1 zucchini (store in fridge)
- **Cook** 1 cup couscous (store in fridge)
- **Open** 2 cans chickpeas + 1 can white beans (rinse, drain, store)
- **Bake** 4 chicken breasts (use 1 tonight, freeze 3)

Now every weeknight meal takes 15-20 minutes instead of 30.
