# Mediterranean Diet Beginner's Guide

The Mediterranean diet isn't a "diet" — it's a way of eating that people in Greece, Italy, and Spain have followed for centuries. It's been ranked **#1 best diet overall** by *U.S. News & World Report* for **seven consecutive years**, and the science behind it is overwhelming: reduced heart disease, lower Type 2 diabetes risk, and longer lifespan.

But here's the good news: **you don't need to live by the sea or eat fancy ingredients** to follow it. This guide shows you exactly what to put on your plate, what to skip, and how to make every meal 400-600 calories with at least 15g protein.

## What to Eat Every Day (The Framework)

The Mediterranean diet follows a simple plate formula:

```
🥗 HALF your plate   = Vegetables + fruits (variety, color)
🍞 A QUARTER         = Whole grains (bread, pasta, rice, couscous)
🐟 A QUARTER         = Protein (fish 2-3x/week, poultry, eggs, legumes, dairy)
🫒 DAILY             = Olive oil (the main fat source)
🌿 HERBS & SPICES    = Instead of salt (oregano, basil, garlic, lemon)
🥜 DAILY             = Nuts + seeds (a small handful)
🍷 OPTIONAL          = Red wine (1 glass/day, with meals)
```

## What to Limit

- **Red meat**: A few times per month, not daily
- **Added sugar**: Minimize (desserts saved for special occasions)
- **Processed food**: Avoid (chips, sugary drinks, packaged snacks)
- **Refined grains**: Replace with whole-grain versions

## 7-Day Shopping List

### Pantry Essentials
- ✅ Extra virgin olive oil (the good stuff — it makes a difference)
- ✅ Canned tomatoes (whole + diced)
- ✅ Canned chickpeas, white beans, lentils
- ✅ Whole-wheat pasta, couscous, brown rice
- ✅ Lemons (lots — Mediterranean cooking uses lemon everywhere)
- ✅ Garlic (you can never have too much)
- ✅ Dried oregano, basil, thyme, bay leaves
- ✅ Sea salt, black pepper
- ✅ Kalamata olives, capers
- ✅ Honey (small amounts for dessert)

### Fresh Weekly
- ✅ Salmon, cod, shrimp, sardines (fish 2-3x/week)
- ✅ Chicken breasts or thighs
- ✅ Eggs
- ✅ Greek yogurt (full-fat is fine — you need the protein)
- ✅ Feta cheese
- ✅ Parmesan
- ✅ Spinach, kale, arugula
- ✅ Tomatoes, cucumbers, bell peppers, zucchini
- ✅ Berries, apples, oranges, lemons
- ✅ Fresh herbs (basil, parsley, mint)

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Mistake #1: Too much pasta**. A Mediterranean dinner plate is half vegetables, not half pasta. Keep pasta to ~1 cup cooked.

❌ **Mistake #2: Skipping the fish**. Fish 2-3x/week is the heart of the Mediterranean diet. If you don't like fish, start with mild cod or salmon.

❌ **Mistake #3: Buying "Mediterranean" packaged foods**. Most are just overpriced junk. Real Mediterranean food is simple whole ingredients.

❌ **Mistake #4: Using vegetable oil instead of olive oil**. The Mediterranean diet's magic comes from extra virgin olive oil specifically — high in polyphenols and heart-healthy fats.

## Why It Works

The Mediterranean diet succeeds because it's:
- **Sustainable** (no counting, no restriction, no "cheat days")
- **Flexible** (you can adapt to any cuisine, any budget)
- **Delicious** (real food, real flavors)
- **Backed by 60+ years of research**

## Your First Week — Action Plan

1. **Saturday morning**: Go shopping using the list above
2. **Sunday afternoon**: Prep 3 things:
 - Cook a big pot of beans (chickpeas + white beans)
 - Make a batch of whole-grain pasta or couscous
 - Roast a tray of vegetables (zucchini, peppers, tomatoes)
3. **Every meal**: Follow the half-quarter-quarter framework
4. **Drink water**, with lemon if you like
5. **Walk 30 minutes daily** (Mediterranean lifestyle, not just diet)
