# AI Disclosure

Healthy Western Recipes uses artificial intelligence (AI) tools to assist with our editorial work. We believe in transparency about how we use these tools.

## Where we use AI

- **Recipe research**: Identifying which Mediterranean or Western dishes fit our 400-600 kcal, 15g+ protein target
- **Outline drafting**: Generating initial recipe outlines based on our editorial briefs
- **Nutrition calculation**: Cross-referencing ingredient databases to compute calories, protein, carbs, fat, and fiber per serving
- **Photography prompts**: Writing image generation prompts for food photography
- **SEO metadata**: Drafting meta descriptions and tags (always human-reviewed)

## Where humans remain in the loop

- **Recipe writing**: Every recipe is rewritten by a human editor from any AI draft
- **Test cooking**: Every recipe is cooked at least twice by a human in our test kitchen
- **Photography**: All photos are shot in our kitchen
- **Editorial review**: Every recipe is reviewed by a human editor before publication
- **Corrections**: All user-submitted corrections are reviewed and processed by humans

## What AI does not do

- We do not publish fully AI-generated recipes without human review and testing
- We do not use AI to impersonate human writers or sources
- We do not use AI to generate fake reviews or testimonials
- We do not use AI to manipulate search rankings (e.g., AI-generated doorway pages)

## Our commitment

We use AI as a tool to help us cook, write, and publish more efficiently. We do not use it to replace the editorial judgment and test cooking that makes a recipe trustworthy. If you ever find a recipe that seems wrong, please [contact us](/about/) — every correction is reviewed by a human.

## Updates to this policy

We will update this disclosure if our use of AI changes substantially. The most recent revision is shown at the top of this page.
