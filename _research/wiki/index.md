## Pipeline
1. **Fetch** (5 recipes/source/day) → `_research/raw/`
2. **Adapt** (LLM transformation, nutrition validation) → `_research/adapted/`
3. **Ingest** (SQLite + markdown export) → `_research/data/recipes.db` + `wiki/{source}/`
4. **Review** (manual publish selection from wiki index) → `_recipes/` Jekyll
