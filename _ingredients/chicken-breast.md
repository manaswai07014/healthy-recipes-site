# Chicken Breast — Healthy Recipes

Chicken breast is one of the most versatile and protein-dense ingredients you can cook with. At just 165 kcal and 23g of protein per 100g, it fits perfectly into a low-calorie, high-protein diet. These four recipes show how a single ingredient can anchor everything from a 20-minute weeknight stir-fry to a slow-simmered Italian soup.

## Buying & Storage

When buying chicken breast, look for **moist, pink flesh** with no off-odors. Pre-packaged chicken should be tightly sealed and ideally packaged within 3 days of slaughter.

**Storage times:**
- Fridge (0-4°C): 1-2 days
- Freezer (-18°C): up to 9 months

For best results, freeze chicken breasts **individually wrapped** so you can thaw only what you need. Always thaw in the refrigerator, never at room temperature.

## Prep Tips

Three techniques make chicken breast reliably juicy:

1. **Brine it**: 30 minutes in 1 tbsp salt + 500ml water keeps the meat moist
2. **Pound it**: even 1.5cm thickness means even cooking
3. **Pat it dry**: dry surface = better sear = more flavor
