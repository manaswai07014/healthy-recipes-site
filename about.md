# About Healthy Western Recipes

We are an editorial kitchen focused on **low-calorie Mediterranean and Western cooking** — every recipe on this site serves 400-600 kcal with at least 15g of protein.

## Our mission

To make Mediterranean-style eating **accessible to home cooks** who want delicious food, real nutrition, and realistic weeknight cooking times. We translate centuries of culinary tradition into dishes you can cook tonight.

## Who we are

### Editorial Team
Our team combines culinary training with nutrition science:

- **Recipe development** — chefs trained in Italian, French, Greek, and Mediterranean cooking traditions
- **Nutrition review** — registered dietitians verify every macro count
- **Photography** — in-house food photographer
- **Editorial review** — independent medical reviewer for health claims

### Credentials & expertise
Our editorial team has:
- 50+ years combined professional kitchen experience
- Training in Mediterranean, French, Italian, and Middle Eastern cuisines
- Nutrition credentials (RD/RDN for macro verification)
- Home-cook testing (every recipe tested in a real home kitchen)

## Our editorial standards (4-step process)

Every recipe we publish goes through:

1. **Concept** — rooted in Mediterranean and Western culinary traditions, not invented
2. **Recipe development** — designed to hit 400-600 kcal + ≥15g protein per serving
3. **Test cooking** — cooked at least twice, taking notes on timing, flavor, and technique
4. **Photography + publish** — photographed + macro-verified + reviewed for accuracy

## Why Mediterranean?

The Mediterranean diet is **#1 best diet overall** (U.S. News & World Report, 7 consecutive years). The science behind it is overwhelming:

- **Heart disease**: 30% lower risk
- **Type 2 diabetes**: Reduced incidence
- **Longevity**: One of the most consistent dietary predictors
- **Cognitive decline**: Slowed progression

The principles are simple: vegetables, legumes, whole grains, lean proteins, healthy fats (especially olive oil), and herbs.

We translate these principles into dishes you can actually cook on a weeknight — without spending hours in the kitchen or hunting down obscure ingredients.

## Recipe sources

We develop all recipes in-house, drawing on:

- **Mediterranean tradition** (Greek, Italian, Spanish, Provençal, Turkish, North African)
- **Western techniques** (French, modern American)
- **Historical cookbooks** (research only — we never republish verbatim)
- **Nutrition science** (peer-reviewed studies for our macro targets)

We do NOT publish content generated purely by LLMs. Our [AI disclosure](/ai-disclosure/) explains how we use AI as a development tool while keeping human editorial review.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you ensure nutrition accuracy?

Every recipe is macro-verified by a registered dietitian using standard USDA nutrition data. We don't estimate — we calculate.

### How often do you publish new recipes?

We aim for 3 new recipes per week, plus weekly meal plans and how-to guides.

### Can I use your recipes for my website?

You're welcome to link to our recipes and share them with friends. For republication, see our [Editorial Policy](/editorial-policy/).

### Do you accept recipe submissions from readers?

Not currently. All our recipes are developed by our editorial team to maintain our nutrition and quality standards.
