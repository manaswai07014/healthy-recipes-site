introduction: |
  The kind of weeknight dinner that makes you feel like a kitchen genius. Everything — chicken, beans, onions, garlic, stock and spinach — cooks in a single pot. The parmesan melts into the broth to create a rich, savory backbone that makes the 560 calories feel much more indulgent than they are. 42g of protein per serving, ready in 30 minutes.
instructions:
  - "Heat the olive oil in a large, deep frying pan or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the onion and cook for 4 minutes until softened. Stir in the garlic and cook for another minute."
  - "Add the diced chicken and cook for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until lightly golden on all sides."
  - "Pour in the chicken stock, add the cannellini beans, basil, salt and pepper. Bring to a simmer and cook for 10 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and the broth has reduced slightly."
  - "Add the spinach in handfuls, stirring until wilted — this should take 1-2 minutes. Remove from the heat."
  - "Stir in the grated parmesan until melted. Taste and adjust seasoning. Serve in shallow bowls with extra parmesan over the top."
chef_tips: |
  This dish is even better the next day — the parmesan flavor deepens and the beans absorb more of the broth. If you don't have cannellini beans, butter beans or even chickpeas work well. For a more substantial meal, serve over 50g of cooked orzo per person (adds about 150 kcal per serving).
