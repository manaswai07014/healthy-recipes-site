# Mussels Marinara over Whole-Wheat Linguine

Succulent Mediterranean mussels simmered in a bright San Marzano tomato and white wine sauce, served over al dente whole-wheat linguine. The trick: steam mussels separately to catch their briny liquor, then add it to the sauce for maximum flavor depth.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 350g whole-wheat linguine
- 750g fresh mussels, cleaned and debearded
- 1.5 cups San Marzano crushed tomatoes
- 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- ½ cup dry white wine
- ¼ cup fresh Italian parsley, chopped
- ½ tsp red pepper flakes
- ½ tsp sea salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper

## Method

1. Step 1: Fill a large pot with salted water and bring to rolling boil. Add linguine and cook 2 minutes less than package directions for al dente texture. Reserve 1 cup pasta water before draining.
2. Step 2: While pasta cooks, inspect mussels: discard any with cracked shells or that stay open when tapped. Pull off any beards with a firm tug. Rinse thoroughly under cold running water.
3. Step 3: Heat olive oil in a large 12-inch skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Add garlic and red pepper flakes, stir for 30 seconds until fragrant—watch carefully to prevent burning.
4. Step 4: Pour in white wine and let it reduce by half, about 2 minutes, creating a aromatic base. Add crushed tomatoes, salt, and pepper. Simmer sauce for 8 minutes until slightly thickened.
5. Step 5: Add cleaned mussels to the sauce in a single layer. Cover tightly and steam for 4-5 minutes, shaking pan occasionally. Mussels are done when they open wide—discard any that remain closed.
6. Step 6: Remove skillet from heat. Add drained linguine and toss vigorously with sauce, adding pasta water a splash at a time if needed to loosen. The sauce should coat each strand.
7. Step 7: Divide among warm bowls, arranging mussels on top. Garnish with fresh parsley and a drizzle of olive oil. Serve immediately with crusty bread for mopping up the liquor.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 520 kcal
- Protein: 32 g
- Carbohydrates: 68 g
- Fat: 14 g
- Fiber: 9 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For extra briny depth, strain the mussel cooking liquid through a fine mesh sieve and add 2-3 tablespoons to the sauce. Substitute clam juice if mussels aren't available. This dish reheats well in a covered skillet over medium-low heat with a splash of water—the pasta will absorb more sauce, so add extra when reheating. Best eaten same day; mussels become rubbery in leftovers.
