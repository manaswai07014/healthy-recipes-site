# Greek Lemon Chicken Souvlaki with Light Tzatziki and Fresh Vegetables

Tender marinated chicken skewers with bright lemon, fragrant oregano, and garlic, served with a cool cucumber-yogurt tzatziki that delivers creaminess without excess calories.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 500g chicken breast, cut into 2-inch cubes
- 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 lemon, juiced and zested
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp sea salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 200g non-fat Greek yogurt
- 1 cucumber, grated and squeezed dry
- 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 1 tbsp fresh dill, chopped
- 1/2 tsp garlic, minced
- 150g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
- 1 cucumber, sliced

## Method

1. Step 1: Combine olive oil, minced garlic, lemon juice, lemon zest, oregano, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper in a large bowl to create the marinade. Whisk until slightly emulsified.
2. Step 2: Add cubed chicken to the marinade and toss to coat evenly. Cover and refrigerate for at least 15 minutes, or up to 2 hours for deeper flavor penetration.
3. Step 3: While chicken marinates, make the tzatziki: combine Greek yogurt, grated cucumber (squeezed dry with clean hands), lemon juice, dill, and minced garlic. Season with salt and pepper. Stir until uniform. Chill until serving.
4. Step 4: Thread marinated chicken onto metal or soaked wooden skewers, about 4-5 pieces per skewer, leaving small gaps between pieces for even cooking.
5. Step 5: Heat a grill pan or cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat. Cook skewers for 3-4 minutes per side, turning until charred grill marks appear and internal temperature reaches 75°C (165°F). Rest for 2 minutes.
6. Step 6: Arrange sliced cucumber, halved cherry tomatoes, and red onion on a platter. Place chicken skewers on top or beside the vegetables.
7. Step 7: Serve with tzatziki dolloped on top or on the side. Garnish with extra lemon wedges and a sprinkle of dried oregano.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 485 kcal
- Protein: 42 g
- Carbohydrates: 18 g
- Fat: 26 g
- Fiber: 3 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For maximum char and smoke flavor, ensure your grill pan is smoking hot before adding skewers. The cucumber in tzatziki releases water as it sits, so squeeze it thoroughly and drain any excess liquid before mixing. Swap chicken for pork tenderloin if desired, cooking to 70°C internal. This dish pairs beautifully with a simple Greek salad or warm pita for additional servings.
