# Smoked Paprika Beef Stuffed Pepper Casserole

A deconstructed stuffed pepper casserole where seasoned ground beef mingles with sweet bell peppers in a smoky tomato broth, finished under the broiler for caramelized edges.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 500g lean ground beef (96% lean)
- 4 mixed-color bell peppers
- 1 can diced tomatoes (400g, no salt added)
- 1 medium yellow onion
- 4 cloves garlic
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 240ml low-sodium chicken broth
- 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- ½ tsp sea salt
- ¼ tsp freshly ground black pepper
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Heat olive oil in a large oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering and fragrant, about 2 minutes.
2. Add ground beef, breaking it into crumbles with a wooden spatula. Cook for 5-6 minutes until browned, with visible caramelization on the bottom—listen for the sizzle to deepen as moisture evaporates.
3. Add diced onion, minced garlic, smoked paprika, and cumin. Stir well to combine. Cook until onion softens and becomes translucent, approximately 3-4 minutes. The aroma should be deeply aromatic and slightly sweet.
4. Pour in diced tomatoes and chicken broth. Stir to create a cohesive sauce. Bring to a gentle simmer—the liquid should barely bubble at the edges.
5. Remove from heat. Slice the bell peppers into thick strips (about 1-inch wide) and nestle them into the beef mixture, alternating colors for visual appeal. Press peppers slightly into the sauce.
6. Cover skillet tightly with foil. Transfer to preheated oven. Bake for 20 minutes until peppers are tender-crisp and beef is fully cooked through (internal temp 74°C/165°F).
7. Remove foil. Place skillet under broiler for 2-3 minutes until the top develops caramelized, slightly charred edges—watch carefully to prevent burning. The surface should have visible browning.
8. Remove from oven. Rest for 3 minutes. Garnish with fresh parsley and serve immediately from the skillet.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 415 kcal
- Protein: 32 g
- Carbohydrates: 18 g
- Fat: 22 g
- Fiber: 5 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For extra protein, stir in 100g cooked quinoa or brown rice before baking—this adds 100 calories per serving but boosts fiber. Swap beef for turkey or chicken thigh for varied flavor profiles. The casserole reheats beautifully for 3 days refrigerated; add a splash of broth when reheating in a covered pan to prevent drying. For spicier kick, add ¼ tsp cayenne or 1 minced jalapeño with the onions.
