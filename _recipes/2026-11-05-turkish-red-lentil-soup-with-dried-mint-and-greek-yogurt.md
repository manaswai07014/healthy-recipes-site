# Turkish Red Lentil Soup with Dried Mint and Greek Yogurt

A velvety Turkish red lentil soup scented with dried mint, finished with a cool swirl of Greek yogurt. Blending while hot creates a silky body without cream.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 1½ cups dried red lentils
- 1 large yellow onion, diced
- 1 medium carrot, diced
- 1 medium potato, diced
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 6 cups low-sodium vegetable broth
- 1 tsp dried mint
- ½ tsp ground cumin
- ¼ tsp red pepper flakes
- ½ tsp salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- 1 cup plain 0% Greek yogurt
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tbsp fresh mint leaves, for garnish

## Method

1. Step 1: Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add onion, carrot, and potato. Cook for 6-8 minutes, stirring often, until the onion turns translucent and the edges of the potato begin to glisten. The kitchen will smell sweet and earthy.
2. Step 2: Push the vegetables to one side. Add tomato paste, cumin, red pepper flakes, and dried mint directly to the hot oil. Fry for 1-2 minutes, mashing the paste into the oil, until it darkens to a brick red and smells nutty. This step builds the soup's backbone.
3. Step 3: Add the rinsed red lentils and vegetable broth. Stir to combine, scraping up any browned bits. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover partially, and simmer for 20-25 minutes. The lentils should collapse completely when pressed against the pot.
4. Step 4: Remove from heat. Use an immersion blender to purée until completely smooth, about 1 minute. If the soup is thicker than you like, add hot water ¼ cup at a time until it flows like heavy cream. Season with salt and black pepper.
5. Step 5: Stir in lemon juice. Taste and adjust: it should be bright, minty, and gently spicy. Add more salt or lemon if needed.
6. Step 6: Ladle into bowls. Top each with a generous spoonful of Greek yogurt and swirl it through the soup with a knife. Scatter fresh mint leaves and a pinch of red pepper flakes.
7. Step 7: Serve immediately with lemon wedges on the side.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 432 kcal
- Protein: 27 g
- Carbohydrates: 63 g
- Fat: 8 g
- Fiber: 10 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For a chunkier texture, blend only half the soup and leave the rest intact. If you don't have dried mint, use 1 tsp dried oregano and add 1 tbsp chopped fresh mint at the end. The soup thickens as it sits; loosen with water or broth when reheating. Store in the fridge up to 4 days, or freeze up to 3 months. Greek yogurt can be stirred in entirely for a creamier, higher-protein bowl.
