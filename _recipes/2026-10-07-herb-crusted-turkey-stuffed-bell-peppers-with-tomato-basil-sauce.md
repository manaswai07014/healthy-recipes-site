# Herb-Crusted Turkey-Stuffed Bell Peppers with Tomato-Basil Sauce

These vibrant bell pepper boats cradle a savory turkey-rice filling beneath a golden parmesan crust. The peppers soften beautifully in the oven, their natural sweetness intensifying as they meld with the tomato sauce.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 4 large bell peppers, mixed colors
- 400g lean ground turkey
- 250g cooked white rice
- 1 can (400g) diced tomatoes, no-salt-added
- 1 medium onion, finely diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 240ml low-sodium chicken broth
- 30g parmesan cheese, grated
- 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp dried basil
- 1/2 tsp sea salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper, freshly ground
- Fresh basil leaves, for garnish

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Cut the tops off the bell peppers and remove seeds and membranes. Slice a thin strip from the base so they stand upright without wobbling. Set aside.
2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Add diced onion and cook, stirring occasionally, until translucent and softened, about 4 minutes. Listen for the gentle sizzle indicating proper temperature.
3. Add minced garlic and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds. The aroma should be potent but not burnt. Immediately add ground turkey, breaking it apart with a wooden spoon.
4. Cook turkey until no longer pink, about 6-7 minutes. Use a spatula to drain excess liquid halfway through. Internal temperature should reach 165°F (74°C) before proceeding.
5. Add canned tomatoes, oregano, basil, salt, and pepper. Stir to combine. Let the mixture simmer for 2 minutes until slightly thickened. Remove from heat.
6. Fold in cooked rice and half the parmesan cheese. The filling should hold together when spooned but remain moist. Taste and adjust seasoning.
7. Stand the hollowed peppers in a baking dish just large enough to hold them upright. Spoon the turkey-rice filling equally into each pepper, mounding slightly on top.
8. Pour chicken broth around the base of the peppers (not over them, or the crust will become soggy). The liquid should come about 1/4 inch up the dish.
9. Cover tightly with foil and bake for 25 minutes. The peppers should be fork-tender when pierced at the base.
10. Remove foil, sprinkle remaining parmesan over each pepper's top. Return to oven, uncovered, for 10-12 minutes until the cheese turns golden and bubbly. Watch for a crisp, golden edge.
11. Rest for 3 minutes before serving. The filling will set slightly and the cheese crust will hold its shape. Garnish with fresh basil leaves.
12. Serve immediately with a side salad for a complete Mediterranean meal. The exposed edges of pepper should show slight caramelization where the cheese met the rim.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 445 kcal
- Protein: 32 g
- Carbohydrates: 42 g
- Fat: 16 g
- Fiber: 5 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For meal prep, assemble peppers in a baking dish, cover tightly, and refrigerate up to 24 hours before baking. Add 10 minutes to cook time if baking from cold. Swap ground turkey for ground chicken or lean ground beef. For extra texture, add 2 tbsp toasted pine nuts to the filling. Leftovers keep in an airtight container for 3 days; reheat at 350°F for 15 minutes covered with foil to prevent drying. The exposed pepper rim develops the most flavor - encourage guests to eat that slightly charred edge.
