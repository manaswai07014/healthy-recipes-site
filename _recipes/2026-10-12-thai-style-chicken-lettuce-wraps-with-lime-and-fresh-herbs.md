# Thai-Style Chicken Lettuce Wraps with Lime and Fresh Herbs

Crisp butter lettuce cups filled with savory-sweet spiced chicken, bright lime, and fresh mint. The chicken is briefly marinated in fish sauce and lime, then stir-fried until caramelized, creating layers of Thai flavor without excess oil.

## Ingredients

(Makes 2 servings)

- 300g ground chicken breast
- 1 head butter lettuce
- 2 limes, juiced
- 3 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 thumb ginger, grated
- 1 shallot, thinly sliced
- 1 fresh red chili, sliced
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp coconut sugar
- 15g fresh mint leaves
- 15g fresh cilantro leaves
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil

## Method

1. Step 1: Prepare the lettuce cups by carefully separating the outer leaves from the butter lettuce head. Rinse and pat completely dry with paper towels. Set aside on a serving platter. The leaves should hold their cup shape.
2. Step 2: Make the dressing by combining 2 tablespoons fresh lime juice, 2 tablespoons fish sauce, 1 tablespoon rice vinegar, and 1 teaspoon coconut sugar in a small bowl. Whisk until sugar dissolves. Set aside.
3. Step 3: Heat 1 tablespoon vegetable oil in a large wok or skillet over high heat until shimmering and nearly smoking. Add the ground chicken, breaking it apart with a wooden spatula. Cook without stirring for 2 minutes to develop a golden crust, then stir and cook another 2 minutes until cooked through (75°C internal). Transfer to a plate.
4. Step 4: In the same wok, reduce heat to medium. Add sesame oil, then garlic, ginger, and shallot. Stir-fry for 30 seconds until fragrant— you should smell sweet aromatics. Add the sliced chili and cook 15 seconds more.
5. Step 5: Return the chicken to the wok. Pour in the prepared dressing and toss everything together over high heat for 1 minute until the sauce reduces slightly and coats the chicken. Remove from heat.
6. Step 6: Stir in the remaining lime juice, fresh mint leaves, and cilantro. Toss gently. The residual heat will slightly wilt the herbs, releasing their oils.
7. Step 7: Spoon the chicken mixture into the prepared lettuce cups. Serve immediately with extra lime wedges on the side. Garnish with extra herbs if desired.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 425 kcal
- Protein: 38 g
- Carbohydrates: 18 g
- Fat: 22 g
- Fiber: 3 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For extra crunch, sprinkle toasted sesame seeds or crushed peanuts on top before serving. Substitute ground turkey or pork for the chicken. If you can't find coconut sugar, brown sugar works perfectly. The lettuce cups can be prepped ahead but fill them just before serving to prevent sogginess. Adjust chili quantity based on heat preference—remove seeds for less kick.
