introduction: |
  When you need dinner on the table in 20 minutes, this stir-fry is the answer. Chicken and broccoli get a hit of garlic, ginger and soy sauce, with a cornstarch coating that gives the chicken that satisfying crisp-soft contrast. The whole dish comes in at 480 kcal with a generous 38g of protein per serving.
instructions:
  - "Toss the diced chicken with the cornstarch and 1 tablespoon of the soy sauce. Let it sit for 5 minutes while you prep the vegetables."
  - "Heat the olive oil in a wok or large frying pan over high heat. Add the chicken in a single layer and sear for 3-4 minutes, turning, until golden on all sides and almost cooked through. Remove to a plate."
  - "Add the broccoli to the pan with a splash of water. Stir-fry for 2-3 minutes until bright green and just tender. Add the garlic and ginger and stir for 30 seconds until fragrant."
  - "Return the chicken to the pan with the remaining soy sauce. Toss everything together for 1 minute to coat and heat through. Drizzle with the sesame oil and scatter the spring onions over the top."
chef_tips: |
  The secret to a good stir-fry is high heat and not overcrowding the pan — cook the chicken in two batches if needed. For an extra-vegetable boost, throw in a handful of sliced shiitake mushrooms or snow peas along with the broccoli. Serve over cauliflower rice for an even lower-carb option (saves about 100 kcal per serving).
