# Italian Turkey-Stuffed Bell Peppers with Tomatoes and Mozzarella

Tender bell peppers filled with savory ground turkey, aromatic herbs, and melted mozzarella. The filling simmers in a light tomato sauce, creating a comforting one-dish dinner with caramelized edges and gooey cheese.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 4 medium bell peppers (any color)
- 500g lean ground turkey
- 1 cup cooked white rice
- 400g canned diced tomatoes
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 60g light mozzarella, shredded
- 30g parmesan, grated
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon dried basil
- 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Cut the tops off the bell peppers and remove seeds and membranes. Chop the tops finely and set aside.
2. Heat 1 tablespoon olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add ground turkey and cook for 5-7 minutes, breaking apart with a wooden spoon, until no longer pink and internal temperature reaches 75°C (165°F). Transfer to a bowl.
3. Add remaining olive oil to the same skillet. Sauté diced onion for 3-4 minutes until translucent and edges begin to caramelize—you want golden color, not burnt. Add minced garlic and cook 30 seconds until fragrant.
4. Add the chopped pepper tops, oregano, basil, red pepper flakes, salt, and pepper. Stir and cook for 2 minutes until softened. Pour in diced tomatoes and simmer for 5 minutes until slightly reduced.
5. Remove from heat. Stir in cooked rice, turkey, and half the parmesan. Mix until combined—the filling should hold together but remain moist.
6. Stand peppers upright in a baking dish just large enough to hold them snugly. Divide filling equally among peppers, pressing down gently and mounding slightly on top.
7. Pour 1/2 cup water into the bottom of the dish. Cover tightly with foil and bake for 25 minutes.
8. Remove foil, top each pepper with mozzarella and remaining parmesan. Bake uncovered for 10-12 minutes until cheese is melted, bubbly, and golden brown in spots. The peppers should be tender but still hold shape.
9. Let rest 3 minutes before serving. The cheese will be dangerously hot—warn your guests.
10. Serve immediately with a side salad for a complete Mediterranean meal.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 478 kcal
- Protein: 35 g
- Carbohydrates: 38 g
- Fat: 19 g
- Fiber: 7 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

Choose bell peppers with flat bottoms so they stand upright in the baking dish. For extra depth, add 1 tablespoon tomato paste with the tomatoes. Swap ground turkey for ground chicken or lean beef if preferred. These peppers keep well refrigerated for 3 days—reheat covered at 180°C for 15 minutes. Freeze assembled (unbaked) peppers for up to 2 months; bake from frozen adding 15-20 minutes to cooking time.
