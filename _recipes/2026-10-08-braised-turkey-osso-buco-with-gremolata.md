# Braised Turkey Osso Buco with Gremolata

Tender turkey cutlets braised in a savory tomato-wine reduction, finished with a bright, herbaceous gremolata that cuts through the richness. The technique mirrors classic Milanese osso buco but collapses cooking time to 30 minutes.

## Ingredients

(Makes 2 servings)

- 300g turkey breast cutlets
- 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 1 small onion, finely diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 medium carrot, finely diced
- 1 celery stalk, finely diced
- 200g canned diced tomatoes
- 120ml dry white wine
- 240ml low-sodium chicken broth
- 1 bay leaf
- 1/4 tsp dried oregano
- 15g fresh parsley (for gremolata)
- 1 tsp lemon zest (for gremolata)
- 1/2 tbsp capers, drained (for gremolata)
- Sea salt and black pepper to taste

## Method

1. Step 1: Pound the turkey cutlets between plastic wrap to an even 1/2-inch thickness. Season generously with salt and pepper on both sides. This ensures even cooking and tender texture.
2. Step 2: Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Sear turkey for 2 minutes per side until golden but not fully cooked. Transfer to a plate and tent with foil. You want golden Maillard browning without overcooking.
3. Step 3: Reduce heat to medium. Add remaining 1 tbsp olive oil to the same pan. Sauté onion, carrot, and celery for 4-5 minutes until softened and translucent, stirring occasionally. The aromatics should release their sweetness.
4. Step 4: Add garlic and oregano, cook for 30 seconds until fragrant. Pour in white wine, scraping up any browned bits (fond) from the bottom—this is where the flavor lives.
5. Step 5: Add tomatoes, chicken broth, and bay leaf. Bring to a gentle simmer. Return turkey to the pan, nestling it into the liquid. Spoon some sauce over the top.
6. Step 6: Cover and simmer for 8-10 minutes until turkey reaches 75°C internally and sauce thickens slightly. The liquid should reduce by about half.
7. Step 7: While turkey rests, make the gremolata: combine finely chopped parsley, lemon zest, minced garlic from gremolata portion, and drained capers in a small bowl.
8. Step 8: Plate turkey, spoon generous amounts of the reduced sauce over each cutlet. Top with a heaping tablespoon of gremolata. The contrast between the warm, savory braise and the bright, cold garnish is essential.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 425 kcal
- Protein: 38 g
- Carbohydrates: 18 g
- Fat: 20 g
- Fiber: 4 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For extra depth, add a pinch of saffron threads to the braising liquid. If turkey cutlets are unavailable, chicken breast sliced horizontally works beautifully. The gremolata can be made 2 hours ahead and refrigerated—bring to room temperature before serving. Store leftover sauce separately; it reheats beautifully and improves after a day in the fridge.
