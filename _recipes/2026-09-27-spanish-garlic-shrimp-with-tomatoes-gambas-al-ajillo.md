# Spanish Garlic Shrimp with Tomatoes (Gambas al Ajillo)

A lighter take on the classic Spanish tapa, where plump shrimp swim in golden garlic-infused oil with burst cherry tomatoes. The shrimp are seared until just opaque, creating a silky texture that contrasts the crispy bread.

## Ingredients

(Makes 2 servings)

- 400g large raw shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 4 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 6 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 2 dried cayenne chilies
- 200g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 30g fresh parsley, chopped
- 80g crusty whole wheat bread
- Flaky sea salt

## Method

1. Pat the shrimp completely dry with paper towels—this ensures proper browning. Set aside at room temperature while preparing other ingredients.
2. Heat a 12-inch stainless steel skillet over medium-high heat for 2 minutes until the pan is hot but not smoking. Add the olive oil and swirl to coat evenly.
3. Add the dried chilies and sliced garlic to the oil. Listen for an immediate sizzle—the garlic should toast for 30-45 seconds until the edges turn golden but the center remains pale. Stir constantly with a wooden spoon to prevent burning. The aroma will become intensely fragrant.
4. Working quickly, add the shrimp in a single layer. Sear without moving for 90 seconds until the underside develops a golden crust. Flip each shrimp and cook for another 60 seconds until just opaque throughout. Remove pan from heat.
5. Add the cherry tomatoes cut-side down. The residual heat will cause them to blister and collapse, releasing their sweet juices. Stir gently to combine.
6. Cut the bread into thick diagonals. Toast under a broiler for 45-60 seconds per side until golden and crisp, or griddle in the same pan with remaining oil for 1 minute per side.
7. Scatter fresh parsley over the shrimp mixture. Serve immediately with the crispy bread for dipping into the aromatic oil.
8. The internal temperature of shrimp should reach 63°C (145°F) when checked with a digital thermometer inserted into the thickest part.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 425 kcal
- Protein: 32 g
- Carbohydrates: 18 g
- Fat: 26 g
- Fiber: 3 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For extra depth, add 1 teaspoon of smoked paprika when you add the tomatoes. If you prefer milder heat, remove the chili seeds before adding. The key to authentic gambas al ajillo is using plenty of garlic—don't skimp. Store any leftover oil (called ajillo oil) in the fridge for up to 3 days; it's excellent for dressing salads or roasting vegetables. To make this dish dairy-free and gluten-free, ensure your bread is certified GF.
