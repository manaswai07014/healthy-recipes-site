# Greek Lamb Burgers with Herb-Feta Yogurt Sauce

Seasoned lamb patties meet a cooling herb-feta yogurt sauce in this Mediterranean-inspired burger. The key is forming uniform patties for even cooking and letting the sauce meld while the meat rests.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 300g lean ground lamb
- 120g low-fat Greek yogurt
- 80g reduced-fat feta cheese
- 2 tbsp fresh oregano, chopped
- 2 tbsp fresh mint, chopped
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/4 cup red onion, finely diced
- 1 cucumber, diced
- 2 tomatoes, sliced
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 4 whole grain burger buns (optional)

## Method

1. Step 1: Make the herb-feta yogurt sauce. In a bowl, combine Greek yogurt, crumbled feta, oregano, mint, parsley, minced garlic, lemon juice, and a pinch of black pepper. Mash with a fork until feta is incorporated but slightly chunky. Cover and refrigerate for at least 15 minutes to let flavors meld.
2. Step 2: Prepare the lamb patties. In a large bowl, combine ground lamb, diced red onion, smoked paprika, cumin, remaining black pepper, and salt. Mix gently with hands—overworking creates tough burgers. Divide into 4 equal portions and form into patties about 1-inch thick, pressing a slight indent in the center to ensure even cooking.
3. Step 3: Heat the cooking surface. Heat a large cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat. Add olive oil and swirl to coat. The oil should shimmer and flow easily when the pan is ready—about 2 minutes.
4. Step 4: Cook the lamb burgers. Place patties in the skillet and cook for 4-5 minutes per side for medium doneness. Look for deep golden-brown crust forming on the bottom edge before flipping. For food safety, ensure internal temperature reaches 74°C (165°F). Rest patties on a plate for 3 minutes.
5. Step 5: Assemble the burgers. Toast buns if using. Layer cucumber and tomato slices on the bottom bun. Top with lamb patty, then generously spoon herb-feta yogurt sauce over the top. Add top bun and serve immediately.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 485 kcal
- Protein: 32 g
- Carbohydrates: 28 g
- Fat: 26 g
- Fiber: 5 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For a gluten-free option, serve the lamb patty over a bed of mixed greens instead of buns. The yogurt sauce also works beautifully as a dip for vegetables or spread for wraps. Make the sauce ahead—it keeps refrigerated for up to 3 days and actually tastes better after resting. If ground lamb is unavailable, lean ground beef or turkey makes a suitable substitute.
