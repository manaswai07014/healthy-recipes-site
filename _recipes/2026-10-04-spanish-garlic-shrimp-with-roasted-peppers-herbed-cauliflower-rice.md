# Spanish Garlic Shrimp with Roasted Peppers & Herbed Cauliflower Rice

A lightened take on classic gambas al ajillo featuring plump shrimp sizzled in aromatic garlic-infused olive oil with sweet roasted peppers and a bed of herbed cauliflower rice.

## Ingredients

(Makes 2 servings)

- 150g raw shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 6 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- ½ tsp red chili flakes
- 100g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 small red bell pepper, sliced
- 1 small yellow bell pepper, sliced
- 60ml dry white wine
- 150g cauliflower rice
- 1 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
- ¼ tsp smoked paprika
- ¼ tsp sea salt
- Pinch of black pepper

## Method

1. Step 1: Pat shrimp completely dry with paper towels. This ensures proper searing and prevents steaming. Season lightly with salt, pepper, and half the smoked paprika. Set aside for 5 minutes to come to room temperature.
2. Step 2: Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add olive oil and swirl to coat. The oil should shimmer and flow easily across the pan—this indicates proper temperature for searing.
3. Step 3: Arrange shrimp in a single layer in the hot oil. Sear without moving for 90 seconds until the underside turns opaque and develops a golden crust. Flip and cook another 60 seconds. Transfer to a plate; shrimp will finish cooking later.
4. Step 4: In the same skillet, add sliced garlic and chili flakes. Sauté for 30-45 seconds, stirring constantly, until the garlic becomes fragrant and turns a pale golden color. Watch carefully—garlic burns quickly and turns bitter.
5. Step 5: Add bell peppers and cook for 3-4 minutes until they begin to soften and char slightly at the edges. The peppers should retain a slight crunch.
6. Step 6: Pour in white wine and use a wooden spoon to scrape up all the browned bits from the bottom—this is the foundation of the sauce's flavor. Let wine reduce by half, about 2 minutes.
7. Step 7: Add cherry tomatoes and the remaining smoked paprika. Cook for 2 minutes until tomatoes begin to collapse and release their juices. The sauce should lightly coat the vegetables.
8. Step 8: Return shrimp to the skillet along with any accumulated juices. Toss everything together and cook for 60-90 seconds until shrimp are fully pink and cooked through. Remove from heat immediately to prevent overcooking.
9. Step 9: While the shrimp cook, steam or microwave cauliflower rice for 3-4 minutes until tender. Season with a drizzle of olive oil and half the parsley.
10. Step 10: Divide cauliflower rice between plates and top with the garlic shrimp and peppers. Garnish with remaining fresh parsley and serve immediately.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 485 kcal
- Protein: 32 g
- Carbohydrates: 18 g
- Fat: 28 g
- Fiber: 5 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For extra depth, finish the dish with a squeeze of fresh lemon juice before serving. If cauliflower rice isn't available, substitute with zucchini noodles or serve with a small portion of crusty whole grain bread to soak up the garlic oil. Store leftovers refrigerated for up to 24 hours; the shrimp are best reheated gently in a skillet rather than microwaved to maintain texture.
