# Three-Bean Mediterranean Tuna Salad with Lemon-Dijon Vinaigrette

Blanched green beans snap against creamy cannellini and chickpeas; a lemon-Dijon vinaigrette sharpens the richness of flaked tuna.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 2 x 400g cans cannellini beans, drained and rinsed (~480g)
- 2 x 400g cans chickpeas, drained and rinsed (~480g)
- 150g green beans, trimmed
- 3 x 145g cans tuna in spring water, drained (~315g)
- 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 1 small red onion (60g), finely sliced
- 200g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 150g cucumber, diced
- 30g Kalamata olives, halved
- 15g capers, rinsed
- 30g feta cheese, crumbled
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1 garlic clove, finely grated
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 15g flat-leaf parsley, chopped
- 1/2 tsp sea salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper

## Method

1. Step 1: Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a rolling boil. Drop in the trimmed green beans and blanch for 3 minutes — they should still squeak between your teeth when bitten, not soften, and their colour should shift from dull olive to vivid jade. Lift them straight into a bowl of iced water for 2 minutes to lock that chlorophyll, then drain, pat completely dry on a tea towel, and cut into 3cm lengths. Wet beans will water down your dressing.
2. Step 2: While the water heats, toss the sliced red onion with 1 tbsp of the red wine vinegar and a pinch of salt in a small bowl. Leave for 10 minutes; the harsh sulphur bite mellows and the slices turn a translucent pink. Drain before using and discard the vinegar.
3. Step 3: Make the vinaigrette. In a jam jar or small bowl, whisk the lemon juice, the remaining 1 tbsp vinegar, Dijon, grated garlic, oregano, 1/2 tsp salt and black pepper. Add the olive oil in a thin stream while whisking hard — the mixture turns cloudy and thickens slightly as it emulsifies. Taste it: it should be sharp and bright, not flat.
4. Step 4: Tip the cannellini beans and chickpeas into a colander and rinse under cold running water for 30 seconds. This washes off the starchy canning liquid that would otherwise dull the dressing. Shake well, then spread on a tea towel and blot dry.
5. Step 5: Drain the tuna and flake it into roughly 2cm chunks with a fork. Resist the urge to mash it to a paste — those larger pieces give the salad structure and let you taste the fish rather than just salt.
6. Step 6: In a large bowl, combine the cannellini, chickpeas, green beans, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, olives, capers and drained pickled onion. Pour over the vinaigrette and fold gently with a rubber spatula, lifting from the bottom, for about 20 seconds — everything should be coated without the beans getting crushed.
7. Step 7: Fold in the tuna last, just three or four turns, so the chunks stay intact. Let the salad sit at room temperature for 15 minutes; the beans absorb the dressing and the flavours knit together.
8. Step 8: Taste and adjust — a squeeze more lemon if it tastes flat, a pinch more salt if it tastes thin. Scatter over the crumbled feta and chopped parsley and serve.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 417 kcal
- Protein: 33 g
- Carbohydrates: 38 g
- Fat: 15 g
- Fiber: 10 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

If you have time, dress the beans and vegetables 2 hours ahead and add the tuna, feta and parsley just before serving — the beans soak up far more flavour, and the tuna stays in clean chunks. Any leftovers keep in a sealed container in the fridge for up to 3 days; the green beans will lose their snap after day one, so if you plan to meal-prep, blanch them a shade firmer and store them separately, folding them in each morning. Tuna packed in olive oil works well too — drain it thoroughly and cut the added oil back to 2 tbsp to keep the calorie count honest. For a vegan version, swap the tuna for 300g of firm tofu pressed, cubed and pan-seared in 1 tsp olive oil until golden, and replace the feta with 2 tbsp of toasted pumpkin seeds; protein drops to roughly 24g per serving. Butter beans or borlotti beans substitute cleanly for the cannellini, and a handful of roasted red pepper or a few shavings of preserved lemon rind stirred through at the end adds a different, sweeter register.
