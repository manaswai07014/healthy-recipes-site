# Moroccan-Spiced Roasted Cauliflower with Herb-Crusted Chicken and Tahini

Caramelized florets kissed with ras el hanout and cumin, paired with juicy herb-crusted chicken, finished with a silky lemon-tahini ribbon.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 600g chicken breast, patted dry
- 1 medium cauliflower, cut into large florets
- 400g canned chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 3 tbsp tahini
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp ras el hanout
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 30g fresh parsley, chopped
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 2 tbsp water (for tahini sauce)

## Method

1. Step 1: Preheat oven to 200°C/400°F. Pat chicken breast completely dry with paper towels — moisture prevents the spice crust from adhering. Season both sides with salt, pepper, and half the ras el hanout.
2. Step 2: Toss cauliflower florets and drained chickpeas in a large bowl with olive oil, remaining ras el hanout, cumin, smoked paprika, and half the minced garlic. Spread in a single layer on a sheet pan.
3. Step 3: Nestle the seasoned chicken breast among the vegetables. Roast for 25 minutes until chicken reaches 75°C internally and cauliflower edges caramelize and turn deeply golden.
4. Step 4: While roasting, whisk tahini with remaining garlic, lemon juice, and 2 tbsp water until smooth and pourable. The mixture will thicken as it sits — add more water if needed.
5. Step 5: Remove pan from oven. Rest chicken for 3 minutes, then slice against the grain into 1cm thick medallions. The internal temperature should hold at 75°C.
6. Step 6: Arrange chicken and roasted vegetables on plates. Drizzle tahini sauce in a zigzag pattern. Garnish with fresh parsley and an extra squeeze of lemon.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 445 kcal
- Protein: 31 g
- Carbohydrates: 32 g
- Fat: 22 g
- Fiber: 9 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For extra char on the cauliflower, switch to broiler for the final 3 minutes — watch closely to prevent burning. Swap chicken for large shrimp (cook 12 minutes at 200°C) or firm tofu (press 30 mins, cube, and roast 25 mins) for variety. Tahini sauce keeps refrigerated for 4 days; thin with water before reusing.
