# Provencal Cod and White Fish Stew with Fennel, Saffron, and Tomatoes

Fennel softens into a sweet, anise-scented base beneath tender cod and saffron-gold broth, with tomatoes adding Provençal depth.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 450g skinless cod fillet, cut into large pieces
- 250g skinless haddock fillet, cut into large pieces
- 1 medium fennel bulb, finely sliced
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 400g can no-salt-added crushed tomatoes
- 250ml low-sodium fish stock
- 120ml dry white wine
- 80ml extra-virgin olive oil
- 1/2 tsp saffron threads
- 1/2 tsp fennel seeds, lightly crushed
- 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
- 1/2 lemon, juiced
- 15g flat-leaf parsley, roughly chopped
- 2 tbsp whole-grain breadcrumbs
- 1/4 tsp sea salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper

## Method

1. Step 1: Pat the cod and haddock dry with paper towels, then season lightly with salt and pepper. Arrange the pieces on a lined tray, cover, and refrigerate while you prepare the broth.
2. Step 2: Crush the fennel seeds between your palms and toast them in a wide saucepan over medium-low heat for 30-45 seconds, until their aroma becomes sweet and pronounced. Add 2 tablespoons of olive oil, the sliced fennel, and onion. Cook for 6-8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the vegetables soften and turn translucent; the fennel should retain a slight resistance.
3. Step 3: Stir in the garlic and red pepper flakes for 30 seconds. Add the wine and let it bubble for 2 minutes, scraping the base of the pan to release the softened vegetable bits. Pour in the tomatoes and fish stock, then add the saffron threads.
4. Step 4: Bring the mixture to a gentle simmer, cover, and cook for 8 minutes. The tomatoes will darken slightly and the broth will take on a golden-orange color. Crush the saffron against the side of the pan with a spoon and stir to distribute it evenly.
5. Step 5: Lower the heat to a bare simmer. Slide in the fish pieces and cook for 5-7 minutes, depending on thickness, until the centers reach 63°C and the fish separates into flakes when nudged with a spoon. Avoid stirring vigorously so the pieces remain intact.
6. Step 6: Remove the pan from the heat. Stir in the lemon juice, remaining olive oil, and parsley. Taste before adding more salt, as the tomatoes and fish vary in saltiness. Serve in shallow bowls with the breadcrumbs scattered over the surface for a lightly crisp contrast.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 438 kcal
- Protein: 42 g
- Carbohydrates: 18 g
- Fat: 19 g
- Fiber: 4 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For an even saffron bloom, steep the threads in 2 tablespoons of warm stock for 5 minutes before adding them to the pan, then pour the infused liquid into the stew. The fish cooks from residual heat after the burner is switched off, so check it at 63°C rather than relying only on the clock; this helps prevent the cod from drying out.

For a gluten-free finish, replace the breadcrumbs with 15g of crushed roasted almonds. The stew keeps for up to 2 days in an airtight container in the refrigerator; reheat gently in a covered saucepan with a splash of stock, adding the fish only if it was stored separately.
