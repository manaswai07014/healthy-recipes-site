# Spiced Chickpea & Herb Couscous Bowls

There's something deeply satisfying about a bowl where every component plays a distinct role. This Mediterranean creation layers fluffy, herb-kissed couscous against crunchy spiced chickpeas, their cumin-forward crust providing textural contrast to the tender grain beneath. A cucumber and tomato salad brings bright, juicy freshness, while a silky tahini dressing ties everything together with its nutty, lemony depth. The salad greens anchor the bowl with a grassy backdrop that lightens the entire composition. These bowls come together in thirty minutes, making them ideal for busy weeknights when you want something nourishing without spending hours at the stove. The chickpeas develop their best crispiness when roasted at high heat until golden-brown—this step is non-negotiable for achieving that satisfying crunch.

## Ingredients

- 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- 3/4 cup whole wheat couscous
- 3/4 tsp kosher salt, divided
- 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 3 tbsp fresh flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped
- 1 small cucumber, diced
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 2 cups mixed salad greens
- 3 tbsp tahini
- 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 1 small clove garlic, minced
- 2-4 tbsp cold water

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Pat chickpeas completely dry with paper towels—removing moisture is essential for achieving crispy edges. Toss with cumin, kosher salt, and 1/2 tbsp olive oil until evenly coated.
2. Spread chickpeas in a single layer on a rimmed baking sheet. Roast 18-22 minutes, shaking the pan once halfway through, until golden-brown and slightly blistered. Watch for deep golden spots rather than just warming through.
3. While chickpeas roast, bring 1 cup water and 1/4 tsp salt to a rolling boil in a small saucepan. Add couscous, stir once, then immediately remove from heat. Cover tightly and let steam 5 minutes undisturbed—this is crucial for light, fluffy texture.
4. Whisk tahini, lemon juice, garlic, remaining 1/2 tsp salt, and 2 tbsp cold water together in a small bowl. The mixture will seize up initially—keep whisking and adding water a tablespoon at a time until you reach a pourable, creamy consistency. Taste and adjust lemon or salt.
5. Fluff the couscous with a fork, then fold in remaining 1/2 tbsp olive oil and chopped parsley. The residual heat will gently wilt the herbs and create fragrant, seasoned grains.
6. Build each bowl: place greens as the foundation, add a generous mound of herbed couscous, arrange crispy chickpeas, cucumber, and tomatoes in distinct sections for visual appeal. Drizzle with 1-2 tbsp tahini dressing per bowl and serve immediately while chickpeas retain their crispness.

## Chef's Tips

The tahini sauce can be made up to 5 days ahead and stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator—the flavors actually deepen overnight. If it thickens in the fridge, simply whisk in a tablespoon of warm water until pourable again. For meal prep, keep all components separate in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; assemble bowls fresh when ready to eat. If you want to bump up the protein further, add a poached or soft-boiled egg on top—the runny yolk makes an excellent sauce. For a spicier version, add a pinch of smoked paprika or a drizzle of harissa to the chickpeas before roasting.
