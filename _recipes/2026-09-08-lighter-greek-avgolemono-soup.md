# Lighter Greek Avgolemono Soup

A bright, comforting avgolemono with tender chicken breast, silky egg-lemon emulsion, and fresh dill. The technique involves tempering eggs into hot broth to create a velvety sauce without curdling.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 400g chicken breast, sliced thin
- 120g orzo pasta
- 1.2L low-sodium chicken stock
- 3 large eggs
- 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 1 medium lemon, zested
- 100g carrots, diced
- 100g celery, diced
- 1/2 medium onion, finely diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 2 tbsp fresh dill, chopped
- Salt and white pepper to taste

## Method

1. Step 1: Pat chicken breast dry with paper towels. Slice against the grain into 1/4-inch thick pieces. Season lightly with salt and set aside. This ensures even cooking and better texture.
2. Step 2: Heat olive oil in a large Dutch oven over medium heat until shimmering. Add diced onion and carrots, sauté for 4 minutes until onion becomes translucent and carrots begin to soften. Add garlic, cook 30 seconds until fragrant—watch for browning.
3. Step 3: Pour in chicken stock and bring to a rolling boil. Add orzo pasta, reduce heat to medium, and cook for 8-10 minutes until orzo is al dente, stirring occasionally to prevent sticking.
4. Step 4: While orzo cooks, prepare the avgolemono base. In a medium bowl, whisk together eggs, lemon juice, lemon zest, and white pepper until slightly frothy. The mixture should be uniform with no streaks.
5. Step 5: Add sliced chicken to the pot. Cook for 3-4 minutes until chicken is just cooked through and reaches 75°C internally. You should see no pink; the pieces will turn opaque and firm.
6. Step 6: Remove pot from heat. This is critical—tempering eggs over direct high heat will cause scrambling. Ladle 1 cup of hot broth into the egg mixture while whisking constantly to prevent curding. The mixture should thicken slightly.
7. Step 7: Slowly pour the tempered egg mixture back into the soup while stirring gently in one direction. The residual heat will create a silky, creamy emulsion. The soup should appear unified, not scrambled.
8. Step 8: Stir in fresh dill. Taste and adjust seasoning with salt and white pepper. Serve immediately in warmed bowls for the best texture—the soup will thicken as it sits.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 428 kcal
- Protein: 25 g
- Carbohydrates: 38 g
- Fat: 18 g
- Fiber: 3 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For extra protein, add 100g cooked chickpeas during the last 2 minutes. If the soup sits and thickens, add a splash of hot stock when reheating to restore consistency. Substitute cauliflower rice for orzo to reduce carbs further. The egg tempering technique is also the secret to French sauces like hollandaise—low heat and constant whisking prevent scrambling.
