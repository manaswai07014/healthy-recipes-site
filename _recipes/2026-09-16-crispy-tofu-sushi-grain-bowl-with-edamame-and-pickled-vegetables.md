# Crispy Tofu Sushi Grain Bowl with Edamame and Pickled Vegetables

A vibrant sushi-inspired grain bowl featuring crispy pan-seared tofu, fluffy sushi rice, protein-rich edamame, and tangy quick-pickled vegetables. The contrast between the crispy tofu, tender rice, and snappy pickled cucumber creates an textural experience that challenges the notion that healthy eating lacks excitement.

## Ingredients

(Makes 2 servings)

- 200g sushi rice
- 150g frozen shelled edamame
- 150g extra-firm tofu
- 1 medium cucumber
- 1 medium carrot
- 1 ripe avocado
- 2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 2 tsp sesame oil
- 5g fresh ginger
- 10g wakame seaweed salad
- 1 tsp black sesame seeds
- 1 tsp white sesame seeds
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp dijon mustard
- 1 tsp honey

## Method

1. Rinse sushi rice thoroughly under cold water until water runs clear. Combine with 300ml water in a pot, bring to boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 18-20 minutes until water is absorbed. Remove from heat and let rest covered for 5 minutes.
2. While rice cooks, press tofu between paper towels for 10 minutes to remove excess moisture. Cut into 1cm cubes and season with a pinch of salt.
3. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a non-stick pan over medium-high heat. Add tofu cubes in a single layer—listen for the sizzle. Cook without moving for 3-4 minutes until golden and crispy on the bottom, then flip and repeat. Remove and set aside.
4. Julienne the carrot into thin matchsticks. Slice cucumber into half-moons. Combine in a bowl with rice vinegar, 1 tsp honey, and a pinch of salt. Let pickle for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
5. Cook edamame according to package directions—typically 4-5 minutes in boiling water. Drain and season with 1 tsp sesame oil and a splash of soy sauce.
6. Make the dressing: whisk together remaining soy sauce, sesame oil, dijon mustard, grated ginger, and 1 tbsp water. Taste and adjust—should be savory with a hint of sweetness.
7. Fluff rice with a fork and divide between bowls. Arrange tofu, edamame, pickled vegetables, and sliced avocado in sections on top. Add a spoonful of wakame salad.
8. Drizzle dressing over the bowl. Garnish with black and white sesame seeds. Serve immediately while tofu retains its crispy exterior.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 485 kcal
- Protein: 22 g
- Carbohydrates: 58 g
- Fat: 19 g
- Fiber: 9 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For extra crispy tofu, toss the cubes in a tablespoon of cornstarch before pan-frying—this creates an additional crackling layer. The pickled vegetables can be prepared up to 3 days ahead and stored in the refrigerator; they actually improve after 24 hours as flavors meld. Substitute cauliflower rice for the sushi rice to reduce calories by approximately 150 per serving while adding extra vegetables. If you prefer heat, add a drizzle of sriracha or sprinkle red pepper flakes before serving.
