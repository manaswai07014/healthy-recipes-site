# Greek Shrimp Spanakorizo with Lemon, Dill, and Feta

Toasting the rice in olive oil before adding broth builds a nutty base; shrimp folded in at the end stay tender. Lemon zest and dill lift the spinach.

## Ingredients

(Makes 3 servings)

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 medium yellow onion, finely chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 cup white basmati rice, rinsed
- 2 1/2 cups low-sodium vegetable broth
- 1 lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 10 oz frozen chopped spinach, thawed and squeezed dry
- 2 oz feta cheese, crumbled
- 1 lemon, zested and juiced
- 1/4 cup fresh dill, chopped
- 1/4 cup fresh flat-leaf parsley, chopped
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 2 scallions, sliced, for garnish

## Method

1. Step 1: Thaw the frozen spinach, then wrap it in a clean kitchen towel and squeeze firmly until no more liquid drips out. Peel and devein the shrimp, pat them dry with paper towels, and refrigerate. Finely chop the onion, mince the garlic, chop the dill and parsley, zest the lemon, and juice it. Measure the broth.
2. Step 2: Heat the olive oil in a large deep skillet over medium heat. Add the onion and cook for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until soft and translucent but not browned. Add the garlic, oregano, and red pepper flakes and cook for 1 minute, until the aroma fills the pan.
3. Step 3: Add the rinsed rice and stir to coat every grain with oil. Toast for 2 minutes, stirring, until the edges of the rice turn translucent and the grains smell nutty. Do not let the garlic burn.
4. Step 4: Pour in the vegetable broth and add 1/2 tsp salt and the black pepper. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes, until the rice is nearly tender and most of the liquid is absorbed.
5. Step 5: Stir in the squeezed spinach. Cook for 3 to 4 minutes, uncovered, until the spinach is heated through and the rice is tender. If the mixture looks dry, add a splash of broth or water.
6. Step 6: Push the rice to the sides of the skillet and arrange the shrimp in a single layer in the center. Cover and cook for 3 to 4 minutes, until the shrimp curl into a C shape, turn opaque pink, and reach 63°C (145°F) on an instant-read thermometer. Do not overcook.
7. Step 7: Remove from heat. Fold in the lemon zest, lemon juice, dill, parsley, and half of the feta. Taste and adjust salt and pepper. The rice should be creamy, the spinach silky, and the shrimp springy.
8. Step 8: Divide among bowls, top with the remaining feta and scallions, and let rest for 2 minutes before serving.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 529 kcal
- Protein: 47 g
- Carbohydrates: 63 g
- Fat: 10 g
- Fiber: 4 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For a chicken version, swap the shrimp for 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken breast cut into 1-inch pieces; sear them in Step 2 after the onion, then remove and stir back in at the end, cooking until the internal temperature reaches 75°C (165°F). Canned chickpeas (1 1/2 cups, drained) also work: add them with the spinach and skip the shrimp step. Brown rice needs about 40 minutes and 3 cups broth, so it will push total time past 45 minutes. Leftovers keep in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; reheat gently in a covered pan with a splash of broth to loosen the rice. Feta can be reduced to 1 oz or omitted to lower fat and calories further.
