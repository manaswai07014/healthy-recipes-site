# Herbed Couscous Bowls with Spiced Chickpeas and Tahini Drizzle

There is something deeply satisfying about a bowl that comes together in under 30 minutes yet tastes like it took all day. This Mediterranean-inspired creation centers on fluffy whole wheat couscous, whose subtle nuttiness pairs beautifully with the warm, earthy notes of cumin-roasted chickpeas. The chickpeas develop a slightly caramelized exterior when tossed in oil and spices, offering a pleasant textural contrast to the tender grains beneath. Bright cherry tomatoes burst with sweetness against the cool crunch of cucumber, while a silky tahini sauce ties everything together with its nutty, slightly tart richness. A scattering of fresh parsley adds a pop of color and a clean, herbal finish. This bowl works equally well served warm or at room temperature, making it ideal for quick weeknight dinners or meal prep sessions when you need something nourishing without the fuss.

## Ingredients

- 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 2 tablespoons olive oil, divided
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
- 3/4 teaspoon kosher salt, divided
- 1 cup whole wheat couscous
- 1 1/4 cups water
- 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, finely chopped
- 1 small cucumber
- 1 pint cherry tomatoes
- 4 cups mixed salad greens
- 3 tablespoons tahini
- 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
- 1 small garlic clove, minced

## Instructions

1. Toss the drained chickpeas with 1 tablespoon olive oil, ground cumin, and 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt in a medium bowl until evenly coated. Set aside to let the flavors meld while you prepare the other components.
2. Bring 1 1/4 cups water and 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt to a rolling boil in a medium saucepan. Add the couscous, stir once, then immediately remove from heat. Cover tightly and allow to steam for exactly 5 minutes. The grains should be tender but still have a slight bite.
3. While the couscous rests, whisk together the tahini, lemon juice, minced garlic, remaining 1 tablespoon olive oil, and 2 to 3 tablespoons cold water until you achieve a pourable, creamy consistency. Season with remaining salt.
4. Fluff the couscous with a fork, then fold in the chopped parsley and a drizzle of olive oil. Peel the cucumber and cut into half-moon slices about 1/4-inch thick. Halve the cherry tomatoes.
5. Divide the salad greens between two large shallow bowls. Create a well in the center and add a generous scoop of herbed couscous. Arrange the spiced chickpeas, cucumber slices, and tomato halves in distinct sections around the grains. Drizzle 2 to 3 tablespoons of tahini sauce over each bowl and serve immediately.

## Chef's Tips

For maximum flavor in the chickpeas, let them marinate in the spice mixture for 10 to 15 minutes before serving, or cook them in a dry skillet over medium-high heat for 3 to 4 minutes to slightly toast the spices and develop a deeper flavor profile. The tahini sauce can be made up to 5 days ahead and stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator; it will thicken as it sits, so thin with a splash of water before using. If you prefer a looser dressing, add water one tablespoon at a time until you reach your desired consistency. Leftovers keep beautifully when stored separately—the couscous, chickpeas, vegetables, and tahini sauce each stay fresh for up to 4 days in the refrigerator. Assemble the bowls fresh each time for the best texture, especially if packing for lunch.
