# Mediterranean Spinach Rice Bowl with Herbed Chicken

A creative reinterpretation of Greek spanakorizo transformed into a complete protein-rich bowl. Tender chicken breast simmers with cauliflower rice, wilted spinach, and tangy feta in a bright lemon-dill broth.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 400g chicken breast, sliced thin
- 400g fresh spinach leaves
- 300g cauliflower rice
- 120g feta cheese, crumbled
- 200ml low-sodium chicken broth
- 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 1 lemon, juiced and zested
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 medium yellow onion, diced
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1/4 cup fresh dill, chopped
- Sea salt and black pepper to taste

## Method

1. Step 1: Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Season chicken slices with salt, pepper, and oregano. Sear for 3-4 minutes per side until golden and cooked through (75°C internal). Remove and set aside.
2. Step 2: In the same pan, add remaining 2 tbsp olive oil. Sauté diced onion for 4-5 minutes until translucent and softened, stirring occasionally.
3. Step 3: Add minced garlic and cumin, cook for 45 seconds until fragrant. The aroma should be warm and slightly nutty.
4. Step 4: Add cauliflower rice, stir to coat in the aromatics. Pour in chicken broth, bring to a gentle simmer. Cover and cook for 8-10 minutes until rice is tender but not mushy.
5. Step 5: Reduce heat to medium-low. Add spinach in batches, stirring until wilted. This takes about 3-4 minutes. The spinach should be bright green and collapsed.
6. Step 6: Return chicken to the pan. Add lemon zest, juice, and half the dill. Gently fold everything together. Cook for 2 minutes to meld flavors.
7. Step 7: Remove from heat. Crumble feta over the top, garnish with remaining fresh dill. The feta should be slightly warm but retain its shape.
8. Step 8: Divide into 4 bowls. The rice should be creamy from the starch, spinach vibrant green, and chicken golden. Serve immediately with extra lemon wedges if desired.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 418 kcal
- Protein: 31 g
- Carbohydrates: 18 g
- Fat: 25 g
- Fiber: 5 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For extra depth, add 1 tbsp tomato paste with the garlic. Swap feta for goat cheese for a tangier profile. This keeps well refrigerated for 3 days — the flavors actually improve overnight as the lemon brightens the dish. To reheat, add a splash of water and warm gently to avoid overcooking the chicken.
