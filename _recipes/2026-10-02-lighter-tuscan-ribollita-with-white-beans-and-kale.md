# Lighter Tuscan Ribollita with White Beans and Kale

A lighter take on ribollita, where cannellini beans create creamy body while kale adds earthy bitterness. The soffritto builds aromatic depth, and a chicken breast boosts protein to 20g per bowl.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 400g canned cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
- 300g chicken breast, cut into 1-inch cubes
- 200g kale (cavolo nero or curly), stems removed, leaves roughly chopped
- 1 medium onion, finely diced
- 2 medium carrots, diced
- 2 celery stalks, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 4 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 2 slices whole wheat bread, torn into pieces
- 1 tsp dried rosemary
- 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1/4 cup parmesan cheese, grated (optional)

## Method

1. Step 1: Heat olive oil in a large Dutch oven over medium heat. Add onion, carrots, and celery. Cook, stirring occasionally, until softened and lightly golden, about 8 minutes. The aromatics should release a sweet, savory fragrance.
2. Step 2: Add garlic, rosemary, and red pepper flakes. Stir for 30 seconds until fragrant—watch for the garlic turning golden, not brown. Add tomato paste and cook for 1 minute, stirring to coat the vegetables.
3. Step 3: Pour in the chicken broth. Bring to a gentle simmer, then add the chicken breast cubes. Cook for 12-15 minutes until chicken reaches 75°C internally and is no longer pink. Remove chicken with a slotted spoon and set aside.
4. Step 4: Add half the cannellini beans to the pot. Use a potato masher or back of a spoon to crush about half of them against the sides—this creates the signature creamy ribollita texture. The liquid should thicken slightly.
5. Step 5: Return the pot to a simmer. Add the torn bread pieces and stir to distribute. The bread will absorb broth and thicken the soup naturally, creating rustic body without cream.
6. Step 6: Add the chopped kale and stir well. Simmer for 5-7 minutes until kale is tender but still has slight bite. The leaves should darken and wilt into the broth.
7. Step 7: Return the cooked chicken to the pot along with the remaining whole beans. Stir gently to combine. Adjust seasoning with salt and pepper. Remove from heat and let rest 5 minutes.
8. Step 8: Serve in deep bowls, topped with a drizzle of olive oil and parmesan cheese if using. The soup should be thick enough to coat a spoon, with beans, kale, and chicken distributed throughout.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 418 kcal
- Protein: 20 g
- Carbohydrates: 52 g
- Fat: 14 g
- Fiber: 12 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For extra depth, add a parmesan rind to the broth while simmering and remove before serving. This infuses subtle umami without added calories. The soup thickens significantly overnight—if meal prepping, add extra broth when reheating. Swap chicken for turkey sausage for a different flavor profile, or make it vegetarian by using vegetable broth and adding 100g extra beans.
