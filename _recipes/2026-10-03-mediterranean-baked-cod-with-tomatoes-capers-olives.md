# Mediterranean Baked Cod with Tomatoes, Capers & Olives

Aromatic cod fillets baked in a skillet with briny capers, meaty olives, and burst cherry tomatoes finished with fresh oregano and bright lemon zest

## Ingredients

(Makes 2 servings)

- 150g cod fillets, skin-on
- 200g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 30g capers, drained
- 60g Kalamata olives, halved
- 50ml extra virgin olive oil
- 4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 60ml dry white wine
- 1 lemon, zested and juiced
- 4 sprigs fresh oregano
- Flaky sea salt and freshly ground black pepper

## Method

1. Step 1: Position rack in center of oven and preheat to 200°C (400°F). Pat cod fillets dry with paper towels and season flesh side generously with salt and pepper. Let rest 5 minutes to come to room temperature.
2. Step 2: Heat olive oil in a 12-inch oven-safe skillet (cast iron ideal) over medium-high heat until shimmering. Place cod skin-side up and sear without moving for 3-4 minutes until golden crust forms—listen for gentle sizzle, avoid high-pitched popping that indicates heat too high.
3. Step 3: Flip cod carefully with spatula (skin should release cleanly). Arrange cherry tomatoes cut-side up around fillets, scatter garlic slices and capers between pieces. Distribute olive halves among tomatoes.
4. Step 4: Pour white wine around the pan edges (not directly over fish—it will splatter). Scatter oregano sprigs over vegetables. Transfer skillet to preheated oven.
5. Step 5: Bake 12-14 minutes until cod flesh flakes easily when pressed and internal temperature reaches 63°C (145°F) on instant-read thermometer inserted thickest part.
6. Step 6: Remove from oven. Squeeze lemon juice over everything, then zest lemon directly onto sauce. The residual heat will轻度 wilt oregano. The tomatoes should be burst and jammy, sauce should be emerald-green from olive oil and herb mingling.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 412 kcal
- Protein: 26 g
- Carbohydrates: 12 g
- Fat: 28 g
- Fiber: 3 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For extra depth, add 1 teaspoon of tomato paste to the pan before baking. If olives are too salty, soak them in warm water 10 minutes before using. Store leftovers refrigerated in an airtight container up to 2 days—reheat gently in a 150°C oven covered with foil to prevent drying. Substitute cod with halibut or sea bass if preferred; adjust cooking time by 2-3 minutes depending on thickness.
