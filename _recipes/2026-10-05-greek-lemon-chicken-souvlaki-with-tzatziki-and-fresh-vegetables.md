# Greek Lemon Chicken Souvlaki with Tzatziki and Fresh Vegetables

Charred lemon-herb chicken skewers with a cool, garlicky cucumber yogurt sauce. The chicken marinates in olive oil, fresh lemon, and oregano—classic Greek flavors that develop depth in just 15 minutes.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 600g chicken breast, cubed
- 4 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 4 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp sea salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 250g Greek yogurt, 0% fat
- 1 large cucumber
- 2 tbsp fresh dill, chopped
- 1 tsp white wine vinegar
- 4 small whole wheat pita breads
- 1 medium red onion
- 1 medium tomato

## Method

1. Step 1: Prepare the chicken by cutting breast into 2.5cm cubes. Place in a bowl and add 2 tbsp olive oil, 3 tbsp lemon juice, 2 minced garlic cloves, 1.5 tsp oregano, salt, and pepper. Toss to coat, cover, and marinate at room temperature for 10-15 minutes while preparing other components.
2. Step 2: Make the tzatziki: Grate cucumber onto a clean kitchen towel, squeeze out excess moisture. Mix yogurt, remaining garlic, 1 tbsp lemon juice, dill, vinegar, and cucumber. Season with salt and pepper. Chill until serving.
3. Step 3: Thread chicken onto 4 metal or soaked wooden skewers, about 4-5 pieces per skewer. Heat a grill pan or cast iron skillet over high heat until smoking. Add remaining olive oil to pan.
4. Step 4: Place skewers in pan, don't crowd. Cook 3-4 minutes per side until internal temperature reaches 75°C (165°F) and exterior has golden-charred marks. Chicken should feel firm and release cleanly from the pan.
5. Step 5: While chicken rests 2 minutes, warm pita breads in the same pan or directly over gas flame for 30 seconds per side. Slice red onion into thin half-moons, dice tomato.
6. Step 6: Serve skewers with tzatziki, warm pita, sliced onion, and tomato. Drizzle remaining marinade over vegetables.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 445 kcal
- Protein: 42 g
- Carbohydrates: 38 g
- Fat: 14 g
- Fiber: 4 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For extra char marks, ensure pan is smoking hot before adding skewers and don't move them until 3 minutes have passed. Swap pita for lettuce cups for a lower-carb version. Tzatziki keeps 3 days refrigerated—add a splash of water to thin if thickened overnight. Chicken can marinate up to 2 hours in refrigerator for deeper flavor.
