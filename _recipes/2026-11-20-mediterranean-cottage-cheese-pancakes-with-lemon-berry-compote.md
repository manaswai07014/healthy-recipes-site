# Mediterranean Cottage Cheese Pancakes with Lemon Berry Compote

Golden-edged pancakes with cloud-soft centers, scented with lemon and filled with tart berries. A whipped cottage cheese batter cooks in minutes.

## Ingredients

(Makes 2 servings)

- 160 g full-fat cottage cheese
- 2 large eggs
- 40 g rolled oats
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1/4 tsp fine sea salt
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 150 g mixed blueberries and raspberries
- 1/2 lemon, zested and juiced
- 1/2 tsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 tbsp plain Greek yogurt
- 1 tbsp chopped pistachios

## Method

1. Step 1: Blend the cottage cheese, eggs, oats, baking powder, salt, vanilla, lemon zest, and 1 tablespoon of lemon juice in a blender until smooth. Let the batter stand for 5 minutes so the oats soften and the mixture loses its raw, grainy edge.
2. Step 2: Place the berries in a small saucepan with the remaining lemon juice and 1 teaspoon of olive oil. Simmer over medium-low heat for 4-6 minutes, stirring once, until the berries release their juices and the sauce becomes lightly syrupy. Keep half for serving.
3. Step 3: Heat a nonstick skillet over medium-low heat and brush it lightly with olive oil. Ladle 3 tablespoons of batter into the pan. Cook for 2-3 minutes, until bubbles open across the surface and the edges look set. Flip and cook for another 1-2 minutes until puffed and golden.
4. Step 4: Repeat with the remaining batter, adjusting the heat if the underside darkens too quickly. Keep finished pancakes warm in a low oven so they retain their soft centers.
5. Step 5: Fold the reserved berry sauce through the Greek yogurt with a spoon. Stack two pancakes on each plate, spoon over the warm berries, and finish with pistachios and a small drizzle of the berry syrup.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 438 kcal
- Protein: 28 g
- Carbohydrates: 39 g
- Fat: 17 g
- Fiber: 6 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For four servings, divide the batter and berry mixture equally; the nutrition figures remain unchanged per serving. For a thicker texture, pulse 10 g of the oats into a coarse flour before blending. Refrigerate leftover pancakes and berry sauce separately for up to 2 days, then warm gently in a covered skillet so the centers soften without drying.
