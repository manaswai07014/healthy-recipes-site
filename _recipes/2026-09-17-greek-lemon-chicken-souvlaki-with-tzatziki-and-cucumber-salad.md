# Greek Lemon Chicken Souvlaki with Tzatziki and Cucumber Salad

Tender chicken breast cubes marinated in bright lemon, garlic, and oregano, then grilled until charred at the edges. Served with cool, garlicky tzatziki and a refreshing cucumber tomato salad.

## Ingredients

(Makes 2 servings)

- 150g chicken breast, cubed
- 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 150g low-fat Greek yogurt
- 1/2 cucumber, diced
- 1 medium tomato, diced
- 1/4 red onion, thinly sliced
- 2 tbsp fresh dill, chopped
- Salt and black pepper to taste
- 4 skewers

## Method

1. Combine lemon juice, minced garlic, oregano, and olive oil in a bowl. Add chicken cubes and toss to coat. Marinate for 15-30 minutes in the refrigerator.
2. While marinating, prepare the tzatziki: mix Greek yogurt, 1 minced garlic clove, half the dill, a pinch of salt, and 1 tsp lemon juice. Set aside.
3. Make the cucumber salad: combine cucumber, tomato, red onion, remaining dill, 1 tsp olive oil, salt and pepper. Toss gently and set aside.
4. Thread marinated chicken onto skewers, leaving small gaps between pieces for even cooking.
5. Heat a grill pan or skillet over medium-high heat. Cook skewers 3-4 minutes per side until internal temperature reaches 75°C (165°F) and edges are charred.
6. Let chicken rest 2 minutes on a plate. Serve with tzatziki drizzle and cucumber salad on the side.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 485 kcal
- Protein: 42 g
- Carbohydrates: 18 g
- Fat: 26 g
- Fiber: 3 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For extra char marks, press the skewers lightly onto the hot pan. The tzatziki tastes better if made 30 minutes ahead—the garlic mellows as it melds with yogurt. Swap cucumber salad for a small portion of whole-wheat pita if you need more carbs. Leftover tzatziki keeps 3 days refrigerated.
