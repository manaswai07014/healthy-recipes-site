# Greek Lemon Herb Chicken Souvlaki with Tzatziki and Horiatiki Salad

Tender marinated chicken skewers charred on a hot grill pan, served with cool garlicky tzatziki and a crisp horiatiki salad. The chicken achieves golden exterior while retaining juicy interior at 75°C internal.

## Ingredients

(Makes 3 servings)

- 180g chicken breast, cubed
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- ½ tsp kosher salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- 2 garlic cloves, minced (divided)
- 150g 2% Greek yogurt
- 1 cucumber, grated and drained
- 2 tbsp fresh dill, chopped
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 cucumber, diced
- ¼ red onion, thinly sliced
- 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1 tsp dried oregano

## Method

1. Step 1: Marinate the chicken - Combine 2 tbsp olive oil, lemon juice, 1 tsp oregano, salt, pepper, and half the minced garlic in a bowl. Add chicken cubes, toss to coat, cover and refrigerate for 15-30 minutes. The acid tenderizes while herbs infuse flavor.
2. Step 2: Make the tzatziki - Grate remaining cucumber, squeeze out excess water with clean towels. Mix Greek yogurt, remaining garlic, dill, 1 tsp olive oil, pinch of salt. Fold in cucumber. Chill until serving. Texture should be thick but pourable.
3. Step 3: Prepare the horiatiki salad - Halve cherry tomatoes, dice cucumber, slice red onion thin. Toss with red wine vinegar, remaining oregano, 1 tbsp olive oil. Let rest 10 minutes. Colors should remain vibrant, onions lose sharp bite.
4. Step 4: Thread and cook chicken - Thread marinated chicken onto metal or soaked wooden skewers. Heat grill pan over high heat until smoking. Cook skewers 3-4 minutes per side, turning with tongs. Internal temp must reach 75°C. Look for golden-char marks and juices running clear.
5. Step 5: Rest and serve - Let skewers rest 2 minutes. Plate over salad, drizzle tzatziki alongside. Garnish with extra dill and lemon wedge. Serve immediately while chicken remains warm.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 485 kcal
- Protein: 38 g
- Carbohydrates: 18 g
- Fat: 28 g
- Fiber: 3 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For extra char, don't move skewers frequently—let them sear. Swap chicken for pork tenderloin (cook to 70°C) or salmon (cook to 63°C). Leftover tzatziki keeps 3 days refrigerated. Add crumbled feta for extra protein but watch calories—each tablespoon adds 25 kcal. If Greek yogurt is too thick, thin with splash of water.
