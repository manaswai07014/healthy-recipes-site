# Tuscan White Bean and Kale Soup with Turkey Sausage

A lighter ribollita where cannellini beans provide creaminess, turkey sausage adds savory protein, and Lacinato kale contributes earthy depth. The soup thickens as it rests, developing complex layers.

## Ingredients

(Makes 3 servings)

- 150g Italian turkey sausage
- 400g canned cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
- 200g Lacinato kale, stems removed, leaves chopped
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 celery stalks, diced
- 1 medium carrot, diced
- 4 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 400g can diced tomatoes
- 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tsp dried rosemary
- 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
- Salt and black pepper to taste

## Method

1. Step 1: Heat olive oil in a large Dutch oven or pot over medium heat until shimmering. Add diced onion, celery, and carrot. Sauté for 5-6 minutes until vegetables soften and onion becomes translucent, stirring occasionally. Listen for the gentle sizzle indicating proper temperature.
2. Step 2: Add minced garlic, dried rosemary, and red pepper flakes. Cook for 1 minute until fragrant—watch for the garlic turning lightly golden, not burnt. The aroma should be pungent and herbal.
3. Step 3: Crumble turkey sausage directly into the pot. Cook for 4-5 minutes, breaking it into small pieces with a wooden spoon. The sausage should be cooked through with no pink remaining, about 75°C internal temperature. The rendered fat will coat the pan bottom with a flavorful fond.
4. Step 4: Pour in diced tomatoes with their juices, scraping up any browned bits from the pan bottom. Add chicken broth and bring to a rolling simmer. Let it bubble gently for 2-3 minutes to meld flavors.
5. Step 5: Add drained cannellini beans and chopped kale. Stir well to distribute. Return to a simmer, then reduce heat to medium-low. Cover partially and cook for 15-18 minutes until kale is tender and beans have begun to break down slightly, thickening the broth naturally.
6. Step 6: Season with salt and pepper. For extra richness, drizzle each serving with an additional 1/2 tsp olive oil. The soup will thicken further as it rests. Serve immediately with crusty whole-grain bread if desired.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 420 kcal
- Protein: 22 g
- Carbohydrates: 45 g
- Fat: 16 g
- Fiber: 11 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For extra protein without adding calories, mash 1/3 of the beans before adding them—the released starches create natural thickness. Leftovers improve overnight as flavors meld; reheat gently on the stovetop with a splash of broth. Swap turkey sausage for chicken sausage or omit entirely for a vegetarian version using vegetable broth and adding 2 tbsp nutritional yeast.
