# Lemon-Cod Foil Packets with Spinach, Cherry Tomatoes & Pine Nuts

Seal cod fillets with spinach, cherry tomatoes, and lemon in parchment or foil packets—the steam gently cooks the fish while infusing it with bright citrus notes and earthy pine nuts.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 600g skinless cod fillets
- 200g baby spinach
- 200g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 30g pine nuts
- 4 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 lemons (1 sliced, 1 juiced)
- 1 tsp sea salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
- 1 tsp dried oregano

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Cut 4 large sheets of parchment paper or aluminum foil, about 30cm each.
2. Pat cod fillets dry with paper towels—moisture on the surface prevents proper browning. Season both sides generously with salt, pepper, and dried oregano.
3. Divide spinach evenly among the 4 parchment sheets, spreading leaves in a single layer to create a bed for the fish.
4. Place one cod fillet on top of the spinach on each sheet. Scatter cherry tomatoes and minced garlic around the fish.
5. Drizzle each packet with 1 tablespoon of olive oil. Squeeze fresh lemon juice over the fish, then top with lemon slices and a generous sprinkle of pine nuts.
6. Fold the parchment over the fish and fold the edges tightly, crimping them to create a sealed packet—steam is essential here, so ensure no gaps.
7. Place packets on a baking sheet and bake for 18-20 minutes at 200°C. The cod should reach 63°C internal temperature and flake easily with a fork.
8. Remove from oven and let rest in packets for 2 minutes—carryover cooking will finish the fish. Open carefully, as hot steam will escape.
9. Transfer to plates, garnish with fresh parsley, and serve immediately with crusty bread or a light side salad if desired.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 425 kcal
- Protein: 38 g
- Carbohydrates: 12 g
- Fat: 26 g
- Fiber: 3 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For extra depth, add 1 tbsp capers to each packet before sealing. The pine nuts toast beautifully in the steam, releasing their buttery aroma—if raw pine nuts are unavailable, lightly toast them in a dry pan first. These packets freeze well assembled (uncooked) for up to 2 months; add 5 minutes to cook time from frozen. Any white fish works—halibut or sea bass are excellent substitutes for cod.
