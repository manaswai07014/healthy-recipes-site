# Provençal Fish Stew with Fennel and Saffron

A fragrant Bouillabaisse-inspired stew where fennel fronds and saffron threads create an aromatic court bouillon. The fish is poached gently at 63°C for silky, translucent flesh.

## Ingredients

(Makes 3 servings)

- 500g firm white fish fillets (cod or halibut), cut into 3cm chunks
- 150g raw shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 200g fennel bulb, thinly sliced, fronds reserved for garnish
- 400g canned diced tomatoes
- 80g yellow onion, finely diced
- 40g leek, white part only, sliced
- 10g garlic, minced
- 1.5 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 80ml dry white wine
- 400ml low-sodium fish stock
- 150g baby potatoes, quartered
- 1 pinch saffron threads
- 2 sprigs fresh thyme
- 1 bay leaf
- 60g crusty bread, for serving

## Method

1. Step 1: Warm the olive oil in a large Dutch oven over medium-low heat. Add the onion and leek, season with a pinch of salt, and sweat for 6-7 minutes until translucent and softened. The aromatics should become fragrant and slightly golden, not browned.
2. Step 2: Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute until aromatic. Pour in the white wine and let it reduce by half, about 2-3 minutes. You should smell the wine's acidity mellow into a sweet base.
3. Step 3: Add the fish stock, canned tomatoes, potatoes, saffron threads, thyme, and bay leaf. Bring to a gentle simmer over medium heat. Let the potatoes cook for 10-12 minutes until nearly tender when pierced with a fork.
4. Step 4: Add the sliced fennel to the pot and simmer for another 4-5 minutes until just tender. The saffron will have released a deep golden color to the broth.
5. Step 5: Gently add the fish chunks and shrimp to the simmering broth. Cook for exactly 4-5 minutes until the fish is opaque and flakes easily, and the shrimp have turned pink. Do not stir vigorously—shake the pot gently to distribute. The fish should reach 63°C internally for perfect doneness.
6. Step 6: Remove from heat immediately. Taste and adjust seasoning with white pepper and a tiny squeeze of lemon if desired. The broth should be golden, aromatic, and lightly seasoned.
7. Step 7: Ladle into deep bowls, garnish with reserved fennel fronds, and serve with crusty bread on the side.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 405 kcal
- Protein: 38 g
- Carbohydrates: 28 g
- Fat: 14 g
- Fiber: 5 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For deeper saffron flavor, steep the threads in 2 tbsp of warm stock for 10 minutes before adding. Swap shrimp for mussels if preferred—just add them in the last 3 minutes of cooking. The stew keeps refrigerated for up to 2 days; add a splash of stock when reheating to restore consistency. For extra protein, increase shrimp to 200g; for fewer carbs, omit the potatoes and increase fish to 550g.
