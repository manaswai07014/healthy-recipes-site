# Greek Lemon Chicken Souvlaki with Tzatziki and Fresh Vegetables

Tender marinated chicken breast threaded onto aromatic rosemary skewers, grilled until golden with a caramelized lemon crust, then served alongside a cool, garlicky tzatziki made with protein-rich Greek yogurt.

## Ingredients

(Makes 3 servings)

- 450g chicken breast, cubed
- 60ml olive oil + 60ml for dressing
- 3 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- Sea salt and black pepper
- 150g non-fat Greek yogurt
- 100g cucumber, grated and drained
- 1 tbsp fresh dill, chopped
- 1 small red onion, sliced
- 200g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar

## Method

1. Step 1: Combine 60ml olive oil, 2 tbsp lemon juice, 2 minced garlic cloves, oregano, smoked paprika, 1/4 tsp salt and pepper in a bowl. Add chicken cubes and massage marinade into meat. Cover and refrigerate for 15-30 minutes — the acid tenderizes while the herbs permeate the fibers.
2. Step 2: While chicken marinates, make tzatziki: wrap grated cucumber in a clean kitchen towel and squeeze out excess moisture (this prevents watery sauce). Mix cucumber with Greek yogurt, remaining lemon juice, 1 minced garlic clove, fresh dill, and a pinch of salt. Chill until serving.
3. Step 3: Thread marinated chicken onto metal or soaked wooden skewers, leaving small gaps between pieces for even cooking. Preheat grill or grill pan to medium-high heat — you should see faint wisps of smoke.
4. Step 4: Grill skewers 3-4 minutes per side, turning once, until internal temperature reaches 75°C (165°F) and exterior shows golden char marks. The sound should transition from sizzling to softer ticking as sugars caramelize.
5. Step 5: Toss red onion slices with red wine vinegar and a pinch of salt while chicken rests — this softens the bite and adds brightness. Halve cherry tomatoes and arrange on a platter.
6. Step 6: Serve chicken skewers over the fresh vegetables with a generous dollop of tzatziki. The contrast between warm, charred meat and cool, creamy sauce is the hallmark of this dish.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 485 kcal
- Protein: 38 g
- Carbohydrates: 12 g
- Fat: 32 g
- Fiber: 2 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For extra char, press skewers flat against the grill for the final 30 seconds of each side. Swap rosemary sprigs for skewers to infuse subtle piney aroma. Make tzatziki ahead — flavors meld overnight. Use full-fat Greek yogurt if calories permit; the texture is impossibly silky. Store leftover tzatziki up to 3 days, though it may loosen as cucumber releases water.
