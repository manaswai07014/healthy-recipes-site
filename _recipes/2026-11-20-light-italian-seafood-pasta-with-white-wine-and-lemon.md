# Light Italian Seafood Pasta with White Wine and Lemon

Succulent shrimp and clams release a briny sweetness into a glossy white wine sauce, brightened with lemon and finished over al dente pasta.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 225 g spaghetti
- 300 g peeled deveined shrimp
- 250 g fresh clams, scrubbed
- 120 ml dry white wine
- 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 small shallot, finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
- 1 lemon, zested and juiced
- 20 g flat-leaf parsley, chopped
- 60 g finely grated Parmesan
- 1/4 tsp fine sea salt
- 1/8 tsp black pepper

## Method

1. Step 1: Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a vigorous boil. Cook the spaghetti for 7 minutes, then add the clams and cook for 3-5 minutes more until the shells open and the spaghetti reaches al dente. Lift out the clams with a slotted spoon, reserving the cooking water. Discard any clams that remain closed.
2. Step 2: Heat the olive oil in a wide sauté pan over medium heat. Add the shallot and cook for 2-3 minutes until translucent and fragrant. Add the garlic and red pepper flakes, stirring for 30 seconds until the garlic smells sweet without browning.
3. Step 3: Pour in the white wine and increase the heat to medium-high. Simmer for 3-4 minutes, scraping the base of the pan, until the alcohol aroma has cooked away and the liquid has reduced slightly.
4. Step 4: Add the shrimp and cook for 2-3 minutes, turning once, until the exterior is pink and the centers are opaque. Add the opened clams and cook for 1 minute, until heated through and steaming. The shrimp must reach 63°C internally; clams should have opened during cooking.
5. Step 5: Add the drained spaghetti to the pan with 60 ml of the reserved clam water. Toss vigorously for 1-2 minutes, adding more pasta water a splash at a time if needed, until the sauce clings to the strands in a light gloss rather than pooling at the bottom.
6. Step 6: Remove the pan from the heat. Stir in the lemon zest, half the lemon juice, the parsley, and the Parmesan. Toss again, then season with salt and pepper. Serve immediately in warmed bowls with the remaining clams arranged around the pasta.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 498 kcal
- Protein: 39 g
- Carbohydrates: 55 g
- Fat: 12 g
- Fiber: 4 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For a more consistent weeknight result, replace the clams with 250 g of extra-virgin olive oil-packed tuna, drained and flaked; add it after the shrimp are cooked so its texture stays moist. If using fresh clams, soak them in cold water with a pinch of salt for 20 minutes before cooking to release sand, and buy them from a reputable fishmonger. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and use within 1 day; reheat gently in a splash of pasta water, adding a fresh squeeze of lemon. Cooked seafood pasta should be reheated to 63°C.
