# Greek Lemon Chicken Souvlaki with Tzatziki and Fresh Herb Salad

Tender marinated chicken skewers with bright lemon, garlic, and oregano, paired with a cool cucumber tzatziki. The chicken reaches 75°C for food safety while remaining juicy—key is not overcooking and letting the marinade do the work.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 500g chicken breast, cubed
- 60ml extra virgin olive oil
- 3 tbsp fresh lemon juice + 1 lemon zest
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- 200g Greek yogurt, non-fat
- 1 medium cucumber, grated and drained
- 2 tbsp fresh dill, chopped
- 1 red bell pepper, chunked
- 1 yellow bell pepper, chunked
- 1 red onion, chunked
- 1/2 tsp sea salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 4 whole wheat pita breads
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1/4 cup red wine vinegar

## Method

1. Step 1: Combine 2 tbsp olive oil, lemon juice, lemon zest, minced garlic, oregano, salt, and pepper in a bowl. Add chicken cubes and toss to coat. Marinate at room temperature for 15 minutes while preparing other components—you'll see the chicken turn opaque at the edges.
2. Step 2: Make the tzatziki: Grate cucumber onto a clean kitchen towel, squeeze out excess water (the key step—this prevents watery sauce). Mix with Greek yogurt, remaining 1 tbsp olive oil, dill, 1 minced garlic clove, pinch of salt. Refrigerate until serving.
3. Step 3: Thread marinated chicken onto metal or soaked wooden skewers, alternating with bell pepper chunks and red onion pieces. Each skewer gets about 4-5 chicken pieces.
4. Step 4: Heat a grill pan or skillet over medium-high heat. Cook skewers 3-4 minutes per side, turning until internal temperature reaches 75°C (165°F). The chicken should have charred grill marks and feel firm but not tough. Rest 2 minutes.
5. Step 5: Quick-pickle the red onions: Toss remaining red onion slices with red wine vinegar and a pinch of salt while chicken rests. They'll soften and turn bright magenta.
6. Step 6: Warm pita breads in the dry skillet for 30 seconds per side until pliable and slightly toasted. Serve chicken skewers with tzatziki, pickled onions, fresh tomatoes, and pita.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 485 kcal
- Protein: 38 g
- Carbohydrates: 42 g
- Fat: 16 g
- Fiber: 6 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For extra smoky flavor, add a pinch of smoked paprika to the chicken marinade. Swap chicken for pork tenderloin if preferred—just cook to 70°C. The tzatziki thickens overnight, so add a splash of lemon juice if too thick. Make extra pickled onions—they keep 5 days refrigerated and add brightness to salads and grain bowls. If Greek yogurt isn't available, strain regular plain yogurt through cheesecloth for 2 hours.
