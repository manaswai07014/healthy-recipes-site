# Greek Lemon Chicken Souvlaki with Tzatziki

Tender marinated chicken thighs grilled until charred and juicy, served with a cool, garlicky cucumber yogurt sauce. The marinade uses lemon zest, oregano, and garlic for bright Mediterranean flavor without excess oil.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 500g boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 60ml extra virgin olive oil
- 6 cloves garlic, minced (divided)
- 2 lemons, zest and juice
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp sweet paprika
- 300g 0% fat Greek yogurt
- 1 English cucumber
- 2 tbsp fresh dill, chopped
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1/2 tsp sea salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1 red onion, quartered into wedges
- 2 bell peppers (red/yellow), chunked
- 200g cherry tomatoes

## Method

1. Step 1: Cut chicken thighs into 3cm cubes. Pat dry with paper towels for better browning. Place in a large bowl.
2. Step 2: Add 3 minced garlic, lemon zest, juice of both lemons, 2 tbsp olive oil, oregano, paprika, salt, and pepper. Massage marinade into chicken. Cover and refrigerate for at least 20 minutes (or up to 2 hours for deeper flavor).
3. Step 3: While chicken marinates, make the tzatziki. Grate cucumber coarsely onto a clean kitchen towel. Squeeze out excess moisture thoroughly— this prevents a watery sauce. Mix cucumber with Greek yogurt, remaining 3 minced garlic, dill, red wine vinegar, pinch of salt, and pepper. Refrigerate until serving.
4. Step 4: Thread marinated chicken onto metal or soaked wooden skewers, alternating with onion wedges and bell pepper chunks. Brush with remaining olive oil from the marinade bowl.
5. Step 5: Heat a grill pan or outdoor grill to medium-high heat (about 200°C/400°F). Grill skewers for 3-4 minutes per side, turning until internal temperature reaches 75°C/167°F and exterior shows charred grill marks.
6. Step 6: During final 2 minutes, add cherry tomatoes to the grill. They should be blistered and soft but still hold shape.
7. Step 7: Rest skewers for 2 minutes. Serve immediately with tzatziki sauce, extra lemon wedges, and a drizzle of olive oil.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 485 kcal
- Protein: 38 g
- Carbohydrates: 22 g
- Fat: 26 g
- Fiber: 4 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For extra char, don't move the skewers too frequently— let them sear. The tzatziki tastes better after resting 30 minutes, allowing garlic and dill to meld. Swap chicken for pork tenderloin or salmon (cook to 63°C). Store tzatziki separately; it keeps 3 days refrigerated. To meal prep, grill skewers and store with fresh vegetables— the tzatziki adds moisture when reheated.
