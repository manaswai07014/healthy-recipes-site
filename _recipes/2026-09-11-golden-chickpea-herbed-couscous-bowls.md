# Golden Chickpea & Herbed Couscous Bowls

There's something deeply satisfying about a bowl that comes together in under 40 minutes yet tastes like it demanded your attention all afternoon. These Mediterranean grain bowls showcase the beautiful contrast of textures that makes this cuisine so compelling—fluffy, herb-scented couscous against the nutty crunch of pan-toasted chickpeas, all brought together by a silky tahini sauce brightened with lemon and garlic. The smoked paprika in the chickpea step adds a subtle depth that elevates the entire dish beyond the ordinary grain bowl. We finish with a shower of fresh parsley and a drizzle of that gorgeous sesame dressing, creating a weeknight dinner that feels worthy of a leisurely Sunday.

## Ingredients

- 200g chickpeas (drained)
- 1 tsp olive oil
- 1/2 tsp cumin, 1/4 tsp smoked paprika, 1/4 tsp sea salt
- 150g whole wheat couscous
- 1/2 tsp sea salt and 1 tsp olive oil for couscous
- 2 tbsp chopped parsley
- 1/2 cucumber, diced
- 150g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 200g mixed salad greens
- 3 tbsp tahini
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 small garlic clove
- 30-45ml cold water

## Instructions

1. Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat. Add the drained chickpeas, olive oil, cumin, smoked paprika, and salt. Toast for 4-5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the chickpeas are golden and slightly crispy at the edges. Remove from heat and set aside.
2. Bring 300ml water to a rolling boil in a medium saucepan with 1/2 teaspoon salt. Add the couscous, stir once, then immediately cover with a tight-fitting lid and remove from heat. Steam for exactly 5 minutes—don't lift the lid. After 5 minutes, uncover and fluff with a fork. Drizzle with olive oil and fold in the chopped parsley. The grains should be tender with a slight bite.
3. Make the tahini dressing: In a small bowl, whisk together the tahini, lemon juice, grated garlic, and cumin until smooth. Add cold water one tablespoon at a time, whisking vigorously, until you reach a pourable consistency like heavy cream. Season with salt and pepper. The sauce should coat a spoon thickly but drizzle easily.
4. Build each bowl: Place 100g salad greens as the base, then add a generous scoop of herbed couscous. Arrange the toasted chickpeas, diced cucumber, and halved tomatoes in sections on top. Drizzle with 2 tablespoons of tahini dressing and serve immediately, passing extra dressing at the table.

## Chef's Tips

For the crispiest chickpeas, make sure they're thoroughly dry before hitting the pan—a quick pat with paper towels does the trick. The couscous cooks entirely off-heat in its steam, so timing matters: exactly 5 minutes yields perfectly distinct grains without mushiness. The tahini sauce thickens as it sits; thin with water as needed just before serving. These components hold well separately for meal prep—store the dressed elements apart and assemble fresh for the best texture. Leftover tahini sauce keeps refrigerated for up to 5 days and makes an excellent dip for raw vegetables or a sandwich spread.
