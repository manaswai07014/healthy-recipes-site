# Lemon-Herb Seared Scallops Over Zucchini Noodles with White Wine Pan Sauce

Plump sea scallops seared until golden with a buttery crust, resting on ribboned zucchini noodles dressed in a bright lemon-white wine pan sauce flecked with fresh dill and parsley.

## Ingredients

(Makes 2 servings)

- 400g sea scallops, cleaned and patted very dry
- 2 medium zucchini, spiralized into noodles
- 1.5 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 lemon, zested and juiced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 shallot, finely diced
- 1/4 cup dry white wine
- 1 tablespoon unsalted butter
- 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped
- 1 tablespoon fresh dill, chopped
- Sea salt and freshly ground black pepper
- Pinch of red pepper flakes

## Method

1. Pat the scallops completely dry with paper towels — this is critical for achieving a golden sear. Season generously with salt, pepper, and a pinch of red pepper flakes on both sides. Let them rest at room temperature while you prepare the zucchini noodles.
2. Spiralize the zucchini into thin noodles. Don't overcrowd the pan — work in batches if needed. Heat 1 tablespoon olive oil in a large non-stick skillet over high heat until it shimmers and just begins to smoke.
3. Carefully place scallops in the pan in a single layer, spacing them apart. Sear without moving for 2 minutes until the bottom develops a deep golden crust. Flip and cook another 1-2 minutes until just opaque throughout. Remove to a plate and tent with foil.
4. Reduce heat to medium. Add remaining olive oil and butter to the pan. Sauté shallots for 1 minute until translucent, scraping up any browned bits. Add garlic and cook 30 seconds until fragrant — watch carefully to prevent burning.
5. Pour in white wine and let it reduce by half, about 2 minutes, swirling the pan. Add lemon juice, zest, and any accumulated juices from the scallop plate. Simmer for 1 minute.
6. Add zucchini noodles and toss gently for 1-2 minutes until just tender but still slightly crisp. Remove from heat. Fold in fresh parsley and dill.
7. Divide zucchini noodles between plates. Top with seared scallops. Drizzle the pan sauce over everything and serve immediately with lemon wedges.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 425 kcal
- Protein: 36 g
- Carbohydrates: 16 g
- Fat: 22 g
- Fiber: 3 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For the best sear, use a cast-iron or stainless steel pan — non-stick won't develop the same golden crust. The scallops should sizzle immediately upon contact. Don't overcrowd the pan or they'll steam instead of sear. If your scallops are particularly large, cut them in half horizontally for more even cooking. Leftovers keep refrigerated for 1 day; the zucchini noodles are best fresh but can be tossed with a little salt and drained if watery.
