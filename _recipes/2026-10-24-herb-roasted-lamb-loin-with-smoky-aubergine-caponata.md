# Herb-Roasted Lamb Loin with Smoky Aubergine Caponata

Caponata is one of Sicily's great gifts to the vegetable drawer — a sweet-sour stew of aubergine, pepper and tomato that tastes far richer than its ingredients suggest. Here it becomes the bed for a lean lamb loin fillet, rubbed with rosemary and garlic and roasted until just pink. The lamb stays juicy because the fillet is small and quick-cooking, and the caponata does the heavy lifting on flavour with briny olives, capers and a hit of balsamic. Wilted spinach and a few roasted new potatoes round out the plate, so you get a proper Sunday-style dinner without the heaviness of a traditional roast. The whole thing comes together in under an hour, and the caponata is even better the next day — if it lasts that long.

## Ingredients

- 3 garlic cloves, 2 sliced and 1 grated
- 2 tsp rapeseed oil, for the caponata
- 1 red onion, cut into wedges
- 1 aubergine, sliced and quartered
- 400 g passata
- 1 green bell pepper, deseeded and sliced
- 6 pitted Kalamata olives, halved and rinsed
- 2 tsp capers, rinsed
- 2 tsp fresh rosemary, finely chopped
- 1 tsp balsamic vinegar
- 200 g baby new potatoes, halved
- 1 tsp rapeseed oil, for the potatoes
- 250 g lean lamb loin fillet, visible fat removed
- 240 g baby spinach
- 1 tbsp fresh parsley, finely chopped (optional)

## Instructions

1. Slice two of the garlic cloves for the caponata and finely grate the third; set the grated garlic aside for the lamb.
2. Warm 2 tsp rapeseed oil in a wide, deep pan over medium heat. Add the onion wedges and cook for 5 minutes until they begin to soften and turn translucent.
3. Stir in the aubergine and cook for another 5 minutes, stirring often, until the pieces start to collapse and take on colour.
4. Pour in the passata, then add the green pepper, olives, capers, 1 tsp of the rosemary and the balsamic vinegar. Cover and simmer gently for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the vegetables are tender and the sauce has thickened.
5. While the caponata simmers, heat the oven to 190C (170C fan). Boil the potatoes in salted water for 10 minutes, then drain well.
6. Mix the grated garlic with the remaining rosemary and a generous grind of black pepper. Rub this mixture all over the lamb fillet.
7. Toss the drained potatoes with 1 tsp rapeseed oil and more black pepper. Place them in a small roasting tin alongside the lamb and roast for 15-20 minutes, until the lamb is cooked to your liking and the potatoes are golden at the edges.
8. Wilt the spinach in a covered pan or microwave, then press out any excess liquid.
9. Stir the sliced garlic into the caponata and cook for 1 minute more. Serve the lamb, whole or sliced, over the caponata with the potatoes and spinach alongside. Scatter with parsley if using.

## Chef's Tips

For a vegetarian version, skip the lamb and fold a drained can of chickpeas into the caponata during the last five minutes of simmering — you'll still hit a respectable protein count. If you prefer your lamb more well-done, roast it for 22-25 minutes, but be careful not to go past that or the lean fillet will dry out. The caponata benefits from a night in the fridge; reheat it gently with a splash of water and taste for vinegar before serving. Leftover lamb is excellent sliced cold in a lunchbox salad with a little olive oil and lemon.
