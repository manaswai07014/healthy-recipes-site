# Greek-Inspired Lamb Burgers with Herb-Feta Yogurt Sauce

A Greek-inspired lamb burger featuring fragrant oregano and cumin, topped with a cooling feta-yogurt sauce brightened with dill and lemon. The patties develop a golden crust while remaining juicy inside.

## Ingredients

(Makes 2 servings)

- 250g lean ground lamb
- 75g non-fat Greek yogurt
- 30g reduced-fat feta cheese
- 1 small cucumber, diced
- 1 medium tomato, sliced
- 30g red onion, thinly sliced
- 2 whole wheat burger buns
- 10ml extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1/4 tsp paprika
- 1 tbsp fresh dill, chopped
- 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- Salt and black pepper to taste

## Method

1. Step 1: Combine ground lamb, 1 minced garlic clove, oregano, cumin, paprika, salt, and pepper in a bowl. Mix gently with hands until just combined—overmixing creates tough burgers. Divide into 2 equal portions and shape into 1-inch thick patties.
2. Step 2: For the sauce, whisk Greek yogurt, crumbled feta, remaining garlic, dill, lemon juice, and a pinch of salt until smooth. The feta should be evenly distributed, creating a creamy sauce with visible herb flecks.
3. Step 3: Heat a cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat. Add olive oil and swirl to coat. Place patties and cook for 4-5 minutes per side until internal temperature reaches 70°C (158°F) and a deep golden-brown crust forms. Listen for the sizzle—constant sound means good browning.
4. Step 4: While patties rest for 2 minutes, toast the buns cut-side down in the same pan for 30 seconds until lightly golden.
5. Step 5: Assemble burgers: spread feta-yogurt sauce on both bun halves, add cucumber and tomato slices, place the lamb patty, and top with red onion rings. Serve immediately.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 485 kcal
- Protein: 32 g
- Carbohydrates: 35 g
- Fat: 24 g
- Fiber: 5 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For extra Mediterranean flair, add a few kalamata olive slices to the sauce. Swap ground lamb for lean ground turkey if preferred—this reduces saturated fat while maintaining protein content. The sauce can be prepared ahead and refrigerated for up to 3 days; the flavors meld beautifully overnight. If buns aren't available, serve the patty over a bed of mixed greens with the toppings as a salad.
