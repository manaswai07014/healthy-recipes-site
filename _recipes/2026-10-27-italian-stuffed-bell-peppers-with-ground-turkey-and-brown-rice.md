# Italian Stuffed Bell Peppers with Ground Turkey and Brown Rice

Roasted bell peppers cradle a savory turkey-rice filling, topped with bubbling mozzarella. Sautéing the turkey first builds a rich base without excess fat.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 4 large bell peppers (any color), tops cut off, seeds removed
- 450 g lean ground turkey (93% lean)
- 1 1/2 cups cooked brown rice
- 1 cup marinara sauce (no sugar added)
- 1/2 cup part-skim shredded mozzarella
- 2 tbsp grated Parmesan
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 cup whole wheat breadcrumbs
- 1 small onion, finely diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp dried Italian herbs
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
- 1/4 cup water
- 2 tbsp fresh basil, torn

## Method

1. Step 1: Preheat oven to 190°C (375°F). Slice the tops off the bell peppers and scoop out seeds and white membranes. If peppers wobble, slice a thin sliver off the bottom to stand them upright. Arrange in a 9x13-inch baking dish. The hollow peppers should smell sweet and grassy.
2. Step 2: Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add onion and cook 3-4 minutes until translucent and soft. Add garlic and cook 30 seconds until fragrant, not browned. Add ground turkey, breaking it up with a wooden spoon. Cook 5-6 minutes until no pink remains and an instant-read thermometer inserted into the thickest part registers 75°C (165°F).
3. Step 3: Stir in Italian herbs, salt, black pepper, and red pepper flakes. Pour in marinara sauce and cook 2 minutes until bubbly. Remove from heat. Fold in cooked brown rice, breadcrumbs, half the mozzarella, and Parmesan. Taste and adjust salt. The filling should be moist but hold together.
4. Step 4: Spoon filling into peppers, packing gently and mounding slightly. Top with remaining mozzarella. Pour water into the bottom of the baking dish. Cover tightly with foil and bake 25 minutes. Remove foil and bake 10-15 minutes more, until cheese is golden and bubbling and peppers are tender when pierced with a knife. Filling should reach 75°C (165°F).
5. Step 5: Rest 5 minutes before serving. Garnish with torn fresh basil. Serve with a simple green salad if desired.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 493 kcal
- Protein: 34 g
- Carbohydrates: 48 g
- Fat: 17 g
- Fiber: 8 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For a lower-carb version, swap cooked brown rice for 1 1/2 cups cauliflower rice, sautéed until dry. Ground chicken or 99% lean turkey works, but add an extra teaspoon of olive oil to keep the filling moist. To store, cool completely and refrigerate in an airtight container up to 3 days. Reheat covered at 160°C (325°F) for 15 minutes. Freeze baked peppers up to 2 months; thaw overnight before reheating.
