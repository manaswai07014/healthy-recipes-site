# Greek-Inspired Lamb Burgers with Herb-Feta Yogurt Sauce

These aren't your average backyard burgers. The lamb is kissed with cumin and oregano, then seared until a deep mahogany crust forms. The sauce tangles bright feta with cooling yogurt and fresh dill— spoonfuls of the Aegean on your plate.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 400g lean ground lamb
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- ½ tsp ground cumin
- ¼ tsp sea salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- 1 small red onion, finely diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 150g non-fat Greek yogurt
- 40g reduced-fat feta cheese, crumbled
- 2 tbsp fresh dill, chopped
- 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 4 whole wheat burger buns
- 1 medium tomato, sliced
- ¼ English cucumber, sliced
- 4 butter lettuce leaves

## Method

1. Step 1: Combine lamb with oregano, cumin, salt, pepper, half the onion, and half the garlic in a bowl. Mix gently with clean hands—overworking creates dense burgers. Divide into 4 equal portions (about 100g each) and shape into patties slightly wider than your buns. Refrigerate for 10 minutes to firm up.
2. Step 2: While patties chill, make the sauce: whisk Greek yogurt, remaining onion, remaining garlic, feta, dill, lemon juice, and a pinch of salt until slightly thickened. Taste and adjust lemon. Cover and refrigerate.
3. Step 3: Heat a large cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat until a drop of water beads and evaporates in 2 seconds. Add olive oil and swirl to coat. Place patties flat and listen for the sizzle—you want an audible hiss, not screaming (too hot) or silence (too cold).
4. Step 4: Sear patties without moving for 3-4 minutes until a deep caramelized crust forms on the bottom. Flip and cook another 3-4 minutes for medium doneness (internal temp 70°C/158°F). Rest in the pan with lid cracked for 2 minutes—this carries over cooking and redistributes juices.
5. Step 5: Toast buns cut-side down in the same skillet for 30 seconds until golden. Build burgers: lettuce on bottom bun, lamb patty, generous spoonful of feta sauce, tomato and cucumber slices, top bun.
6. Step 6: Serve immediately with extra sauce on the side. The sauce should be thick but spoonable—runny means under-drained yogurt or too much lemon.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 485 kcal
- Protein: 32 g
- Carbohydrates: 38 g
- Fat: 22 g
- Fiber: 6 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For a lighter approach, serve open-faced over lettuce with all toppings and a generous sauce drizzle instead of a bun. The sauce works beautifully with grilled chicken or as a dip for vegetables. Make extra sauce and store refrigerated for up to 3 days—it actually deepens in flavor as the garlic mellows. If feta feels too salty, soak in cold water for 10 minutes before crumbling.
