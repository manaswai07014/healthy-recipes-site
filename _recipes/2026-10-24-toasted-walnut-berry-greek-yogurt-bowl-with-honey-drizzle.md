# Toasted Walnut & Berry Greek Yogurt Bowl with Honey Drizzle

Toasting walnuts until nutty and warm transforms a yogurt bowl. Cold, tangy Greek yogurt meets juicy berries, crisp walnuts, and floral honey.

## Ingredients

(Makes 2 servings)

- 2 cups (480g) plain nonfat Greek yogurt
- 1/2 cup (60g) walnut halves
- 1 cup (150g) mixed fresh berries (strawberries, blueberries, raspberries)
- 2 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tsp lemon zest

## Method

1. Step 1: Toast the walnuts: Place walnuts in a dry skillet over medium heat. Cook, stirring frequently, until they smell nutty and are lightly browned, 3-5 minutes. Watch closely—they can burn quickly. Transfer to a plate to cool, then roughly chop.
2. Step 2: In a medium bowl, stir together the Greek yogurt, vanilla extract, cinnamon, and lemon zest until smooth. Taste and adjust cinnamon if desired.
3. Step 3: Divide the yogurt mixture between two bowls. Top each with mixed berries and toasted walnuts.
4. Step 4: Drizzle 1 tablespoon of honey over each bowl. Serve immediately, while the walnuts are still crisp.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 435 kcal
- Protein: 29 g
- Carbohydrates: 39 g
- Fat: 20 g
- Fiber: 4 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For a warmer contrast, gently warm the honey for 10 seconds in the microwave before drizzling. If using frozen berries, thaw and drain well to avoid watering down the yogurt. Swap walnuts for pecans or almonds, or add 1 tablespoon chia seeds for extra fiber (adjust calories). Assemble just before serving; store components separately in the fridge for up to 3 days.
