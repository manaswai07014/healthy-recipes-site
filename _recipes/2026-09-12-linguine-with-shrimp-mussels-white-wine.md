# Linguine with Shrimp Mussels White Wine

A briny, elegant pasta where shrimp and mussels release their liquor into a reduced white wine sauce. The linguine finishes in the pan, absorbing the aromatic seafood essence.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 240g dried linguine
- 600g raw shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 400g fresh mussels, scrubbed
- 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 small shallot, finely diced
- 150ml dry white wine
- 200g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 tsp red pepper flakes
- 30g fresh parsley, chopped
- 1 lemon, zested and juiced
- 240ml low-sodium seafood or chicken stock

## Method

1. Step 1: Bring a large pot of salted water to boil. Add linguine and cook 2 minutes short of al dente (about 8-10 minutes). Reserve 240ml pasta water before draining.
2. Step 2: While pasta cooks, heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Add shallots and cook until translucent, about 2 minutes. Add garlic and red pepper flakes, stir until fragrant, 30 seconds—watch closely to prevent burning.
3. Step 3: Add shrimp in single layer. Sear without moving for 90 seconds until底部 is golden. Flip and cook another 60 seconds until pink but not fully cooked through. Transfer to a plate.
4. Step 4: Add mussels to the same pan. Pour in white wine and stock. Cover and steam for 3-4 minutes until mussels open. Discard any closed shells. Remove mussels and set aside with shrimp.
5. Step 5: Add cherry tomatoes to the pan sauce. Simmer until softened, about 3 minutes. The sauce should reduce by roughly one-third.
6. Step 6: Return shrimp and mussels to pan. Add drained linguine and toss everything together over medium heat. Add reserved pasta water gradually to create a light, emulsified sauce that coats each strand.
7. Step 7: Remove from heat. Add lemon zest, half the parsley, and a squeeze of lemon juice. Toss vigorously to incorporate. The residual heat will finish cooking the shrimp to proper temperature (63°C internal).
8. Step 8: Divide among bowls. Garnish with remaining parsley and an extra lemon wedge. Serve immediately while the sauce still clings to the pasta.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 485 kcal
- Protein: 38 g
- Carbohydrates: 52 g
- Fat: 14 g
- Fiber: 4 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For extra depth, add 1 teaspoon of tomato paste with the tomatoes. Swap mussel broth for white wine in step 4 if you prefer stronger wine character. This dish keeps refrigerated for 2 days—reheat gently in a covered pan with a splash of water to loosen the sauce. If chickpea or lentil pasta is unavailable, whole wheat linguine works similarly with slightly more fiber.
