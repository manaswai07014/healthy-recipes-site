# Italian Stuffed Bell Peppers with Ground Turkey, Quinoa & Mozzarella

These vibrant bell pepper halves cradle a savory turkey-quinoa filling finished with bubbling mozzarella. The key: par-baking the peppers briefly so they hold their shape while the filling cooks through perfectly.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 4 large bell peppers, mixed colors
- 600g lean ground turkey
- 185g cooked quinoa
- 120g diced tomatoes, no salt added
- 1 medium onion, finely diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp dried basil
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 large egg
- 60g low-fat mozzarella, shredded
- 20ml extra virgin olive oil
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper

## Method

1. Step 1: Preheat oven to 200°C/400°F. Line a baking dish with parchment. Cut peppers in half lengthwise, remove seeds and membranes. Brush cut sides with 10ml olive oil, season with salt. Roast cut-side down for 12 minutes until slightly softened but not fully cooked—they should still hold their shape.
2. Step 2: While peppers roast, heat remaining 10ml olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add onion, cook 4 minutes until translucent with golden edges, stirring occasionally. Add garlic, cook 1 minute until fragrant but not browned.
3. Step 3: Add ground turkey to the skillet, break into large chunks. Cook 6-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until turkey reaches 75°C internal and loses all pink color. Drain excess liquid if needed.
4. Step 4: Remove from heat. Stir in cooked quinoa, diced tomatoes, oregano, basil, smoked paprika, and egg. Mix until combined. Season generously with black pepper and adjust salt to taste. Filling should be moist but not wet.
5. Step 5: Divide filling equally among pepper halves, mounding slightly. Top each with shredded mozzarella, pressing lightly to adhere. Bake 18-20 minutes until peppers are tender-crisp, filling is heated through, and cheese is golden and bubbling.
6. Step 6: Rest 3 minutes before serving. The cheese should be set and slightly crispy on edges. Peppers should yield easily when pierced with a fork.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 412 kcal
- Protein: 43 g
- Carbohydrates: 28 g
- Fat: 15 g
- Fiber: 5 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For meal prep, assemble unbaked peppers in a freezer-safe dish, cover tightly, and freeze up to 2 months. Thaw overnight in refrigerator, then add 10-15 minutes to baking time. Swap mozzarella for feta for a tangier profile, or add 30g sun-dried tomatoes to the filling for extra Mediterranean depth. Leftovers keep refrigerated for 3 days—reheat in a 180°C oven for 12 minutes to preserve texture.
