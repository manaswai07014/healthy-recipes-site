# Protein-Rich Tuscan White Bean and Kale Ribollita

Cannellini beans simmer into a velvety broth with tomato and kale, while Parmesan adds depth. A final toast of whole-grain bread turns leftovers into ribollita.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 medium onion, finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 1 carrot, diced
- 1 celery stalk, diced
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
- 3 cups no-salt-added cannellini beans, drained
- 1 1/2 cups low-sodium vegetable stock
- 1 1/2 cups water
- 1/2 cup crushed tomatoes
- 2 cups chopped Tuscan kale, stems removed
- 1/4 cup finely grated Parmesan, plus more to serve
- 1/2 tsp ground black pepper
- 2 slices whole-grain bread, torn into pieces
- 1 tbsp chopped fresh parsley

## Method

1. Step 1: Heat the olive oil in a heavy saucepan over medium heat. Add the onion, carrot, and celery with a pinch of salt. Cook for 6-8 minutes, stirring often, until the vegetables soften and the onion turns translucent without browning.
2. Step 2: Add the garlic, oregano, and red pepper flakes. Stir for 45-60 seconds until the garlic smells fragrant but remains pale, preventing a bitter flavor.
3. Step 3: Add the beans, stock, water, and crushed tomatoes. Bring to a gentle boil, then reduce the heat and simmer uncovered for 12 minutes. Crush about one-third of the beans against the side of the pot with a spoon; their starch will thicken the broth into a lightly creamy texture.
4. Step 4: Stir in the kale and black pepper. Simmer for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the kale is tender and dark green. Add a splash of water if the broth becomes thicker than you prefer.
5. Step 5: Remove the pan from the heat and stir in the Parmesan. Taste for seasoning, then ladle into four warm bowls and finish with parsley and additional Parmesan.
6. Step 6: For a ribollita-style finish, toast the torn bread in a dry skillet for 3-4 minutes until crisp at the edges. Add one slice to each bowl and spoon broth over it so it softens slightly while retaining some crunch.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 428 kcal
- Protein: 19 g
- Carbohydrates: 59 g
- Fat: 12 g
- Fiber: 15 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For a thicker, more restorative ribollita, cool the soup and refrigerate it overnight, then reheat it with a splash of water the next day; the beans and broth develop a rounder flavor. To increase protein without changing the character of the dish, serve the soup with grilled chicken breast cooked to 75°C internal temperature, or add a boiled egg. Keep the bread separate for the best texture, and store refrigerated soup for up to 3 days.
