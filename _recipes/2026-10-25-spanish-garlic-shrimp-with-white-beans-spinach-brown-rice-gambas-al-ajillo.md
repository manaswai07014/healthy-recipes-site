# Spanish Garlic Shrimp with White Beans, Spinach & Brown Rice (Gambas al Ajillo)

Sizzling garlic and smoked paprika perfume the oil before shrimp hit the pan, creating a glossy, coral-red sauce. Tossing with white beans and spinach keeps it light.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 600 g large shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 6 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp red chili flakes
- 1/4 cup dry white wine
- 1 tbsp sherry vinegar
- 2 cups cooked cannellini beans
- 4 cups baby spinach
- 2 cups cooked brown rice
- 1 lemon, cut into wedges
- 2 tbsp chopped fresh parsley
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper

## Method

1. Step 1: Pat the shrimp dry with paper towels and season with 1/2 tsp salt and 1/4 tsp black pepper. Set aside. Thinly slice the garlic, drain and rinse the beans, and measure the wine, vinegar, paprika, and chili flakes.
2. Step 2: Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the garlic and chili flakes. Cook for 30–45 seconds, until the garlic is fragrant and barely golden — do not let it brown or it will turn bitter. Use a slotted spoon to transfer the garlic to a plate.
3. Step 3: Increase the heat to medium-high and add the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil. Add the shrimp in a single layer. Cook for 1–2 minutes per side, until the shrimp are opaque, firm, and curl into a C shape. They should reach an internal temperature of 63°C (145°F). Transfer the shrimp to the plate with the garlic.
4. Step 4: Pour the white wine into the hot pan and simmer for 1 minute, scraping up any browned bits. Stir in the smoked paprika and sherry vinegar, then simmer for 30 seconds until slightly reduced.
5. Step 5: Add the beans and spinach to the pan. Toss for 2–3 minutes, until the spinach wilts and the beans are heated through. Season with the remaining 1/2 tsp salt and 1/4 tsp black pepper.
6. Step 6: Return the shrimp and garlic to the pan. Toss gently for 30 seconds to coat. Squeeze half the lemon over the top, then taste and adjust the seasoning.
7. Step 7: Serve over 1/2 cup cooked brown rice per person. Garnish with chopped parsley and lemon wedges.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 472 kcal
- Protein: 41 g
- Carbohydrates: 49 g
- Fat: 9 g
- Fiber: 8 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For a smokier profile, add a pinch of smoked paprika off the heat so it doesn't burn. If you prefer a thicker sauce, mash a few white beans into the pan before adding the shrimp back. Swap spinach for kale, but cook it 2 minutes longer. Store leftovers in an airtight container for up to 3 days; reheat gently in a skillet with a splash of water to prevent the shrimp from toughening. To keep it gluten-free, ensure the wine and vinegar are certified. For a lower-carb version, serve over cauliflower rice instead of brown rice, but add 1/4 avocado per serving to reach 400 kcal.
