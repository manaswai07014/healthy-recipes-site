# Mediterranean Garlic Shrimp Bowl with Cauliflower Rice

A lighter take on classic gambas al ajillo, where the sizzling garlic oil coats plump shrimp served over fluffy cauliflower rice. The aroma of toasted garlic and chili fills your kitchen—it's absolutely intoxicating.

## Ingredients

(Makes 2 servings)

- 250g raw tiger prawns
- 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 1 head garlic, thinly sliced
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 0.5 tsp dried chili flakes
- 1 cup cauliflower rice
- 150g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 0.5 tsp sea salt
- 0.25 tsp black pepper

## Method

1. Step 1: Pat the prawns completely dry with paper towels. This ensures they sear rather than steam. Season with half the salt, pepper, and smoked paprika. Set aside for 5 minutes to come to room temperature.
2. Step 2: Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat for 2 minutes. Add 2 tbsp olive oil—when it shimmers and flows easily, add the sliced garlic. Toast until golden brown, about 2 minutes. Listen for the gentle sizzle; lower heat if the garlic browns too quickly.
3. Step 3: Add the chili flakes and immediately add the prawns in a single layer. Sear without moving for 90 seconds until the bottoms are opaque and caramelized. Flip and cook another 60 seconds. The shrimp should be pink throughout with a slight golden edge. Transfer to a plate, leaving the garlic oil in the pan.
4. Step 4: Add the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil to the pan. Add cherry tomatoes cut-side down. Cook for 2 minutes until softened and slightly blistered. Pour in the lemon juice, scraping up any browned bits—this is the fond, and it packs tremendous flavor.
5. Step 5: While the tomatoes cook, heat the cauliflower rice in the microwave for 2 minutes or steam for 4 minutes until tender but not mushy. Season with the remaining salt.
6. Step 6: Divide cauliflower rice between two bowls. Top with the garlic shrimp and blistered tomatoes. Spoon the garlic-lemon oil sauce generously over everything. Garnish with fresh parsley and serve immediately.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 590 kcal
- Protein: 40 g
- Carbohydrates: 18 g
- Fat: 42 g
- Fiber: 4 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For extra depth, finish with a pinch of flaky sea salt and a drizzle of high-quality olive oil. The prawns can be swapped for sea bass or cod—adjust cooking time to 2 minutes per side. Store leftovers separately: shrimp and sauce keep refrigerated for 2 days, but cauliflower rice is best fresh. To reheat, warm the shrimp and sauce in a pan over medium heat until sizzling.
