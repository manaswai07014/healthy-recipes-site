# Light Provençal Beef and Mushroom Daube with Herbed Polenta

A streamlined Provençal daube featuring tender beef medallions and earthy cremini mushrooms braised in a crimson wine and herb reduction. The technique involves quick-searing beef for Maillard complexity, then building a flavor base with sofrito vegetables before the brief simmer.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 150g lean beef chuck, trimmed and sliced 1cm thick
- 200g cremini mushrooms, quartered
- 1 medium carrot, julienned
- 1 medium onion, finely diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 60ml dry red wine
- 240ml low-sodium beef broth
- 1 can (400g) diced tomatoes
- 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tsp fresh thyme
- 1/2 tsp dried Herbes de Provence
- 1 bay leaf
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 120g quick-cook polenta
- 15g fresh parsley, chopped

## Method

1. Step 1: Pat beef dry with paper towels, then season generously with salt and pepper. Heat olive oil in a large sauté pan over medium-high heat until shimmering—you want a hard sear. Cook beef in single layer for 45 seconds per side until deep mahogany crust forms. Transfer to a plate, juices reserved.
2. Step 2: Reduce heat to medium. Add onion and carrot to the same pan, sauté for 4 minutes until softened and onion turns translucent. Listen for the gentle sizzle—add garlic and cook 30 seconds until fragrant, stirring constantly to prevent burning.
3. Step 3: Pour in red wine, using a wooden spoon to scrape up all the browned bits (the fond). Let wine reduce by half, about 2 minutes—you'll see the mixture thicken slightly and smell intensely aromatic.
4. Step 4: Add beef broth, diced tomatoes, Herbes de Provence, thyme, and bay leaf. Bring to a gentle simmer. Return beef and any accumulated juices to the pan. Cover and cook for 18 minutes, stirring once halfway.
5. Step 5: While beef simmers, bring 480ml water to boil in a separate pot. Add a pinch of salt. Slowly whisk in polenta in a steady stream. Cook 3-4 minutes, stirring frequently, until thickened. Remove from heat, cover, let rest 2 minutes.
6. Step 6: Add mushrooms to the daube in the last 6 minutes of cooking. They should be tender but hold their shape. Taste and adjust seasoning—add pepper and more salt if needed.
7. Step 7: To serve, spoon creamy polenta into warm bowls. Top with the daube, ensuring each portion gets beef, mushrooms, and sauce. Garnish with fresh parsley and a final grind of black pepper. The sauce should coat a spoon.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 485 kcal
- Protein: 32 g
- Carbohydrates: 38 g
- Fat: 18 g
- Fiber: 6 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For extra depth, marinate the beef in 1 tbsp red wine vinegar and a pinch of salt for 20 minutes before cooking—this tenderizes and adds brightness. Swap cremini for oyster mushrooms for earthier flavor, or add 100g frozen peas in the final 2 minutes for color and extra fiber. Store leftovers in an airtight container refrigerated for up to 3 days; the flavors meld beautifully overnight. Reheat gently in a covered pan with a splash of broth to loosen the sauce.
