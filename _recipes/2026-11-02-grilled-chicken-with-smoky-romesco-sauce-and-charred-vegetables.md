# Grilled Chicken with Smoky Romesco Sauce and Charred Vegetables

Charred zucchini and peppers meet smoky, nutty romesco in this Spanish-inspired plate. Grilling the chicken to 75°C keeps it juicy while the sauce comes together in a food processor.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 4 boneless skinless chicken breasts (about 140 g each)
- 1 cup jarred roasted red peppers, drained
- 1/2 cup blanched almonds
- 2 garlic cloves
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tbsp sherry vinegar
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
- 1 1/2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil (for romesco)
- 1 slice whole wheat bread
- 2 medium zucchini
- 2 red bell peppers
- 1 red onion
- 1 bunch asparagus
- 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil (for grilling)
- Salt and black pepper to taste

## Method

1. Step 1: Preheat a grill or grill pan over medium-high heat to about 200°C (400°F). If using an oven, preheat to 220°C (425°F) and line a sheet pan with parchment.
2. Step 2: Make the romesco. In a food processor, combine the roasted red peppers, almonds, garlic, tomato paste, sherry vinegar, smoked paprika, cayenne, and bread. Pulse until finely chopped, scraping down the sides. With the motor running, drizzle in 1 1/2 tbsp olive oil until the sauce is thick but spoonable. Season with salt. It should smell smoky and nutty; set aside.
3. Step 3: Prep the vegetables. Slice zucchini lengthwise into 1/2-inch planks. Core and quarter the bell peppers. Cut the red onion into thick wedges. Trim the asparagus. Toss all vegetables with 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, and pepper.
4. Step 4: Grill the vegetables. Place them on the grill in a single layer. Cook zucchini 3–4 minutes per side until charred and tender. Bell peppers 4–5 minutes per side until the skin blisters. Onion wedges 3–4 minutes per side. Asparagus 2–3 minutes, turning once. You will hear a steady sizzle and see dark grill marks. Transfer to a platter.
5. Step 5: Grill the chicken. Pat the chicken dry and season with salt and pepper. Grill over direct heat for 6–7 minutes per side, until the internal temperature reaches 75°C (165°F) and the juices run clear. Let rest 5 minutes, then slice against the grain.
6. Step 6: Assemble. Spread a generous spoonful of romesco on each plate. Top with sliced chicken and grilled vegetables. Serve extra romesco on the side.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 456 kcal
- Protein: 41 g
- Carbohydrates: 23 g
- Fat: 21 g
- Fiber: 7 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For a smokier romesco, roast your own red peppers under a broiler until blackened, steam them in a covered bowl for 10 minutes, then peel. Swap almonds for hazelnuts, or add 1/2 cup cooked white beans for a creamier sauce with extra protein and fiber. Romesco keeps in the fridge for up to 5 days; bring to room temperature before serving. The chicken can be replaced with firm white fish (cook to 63°C) or pork tenderloin (cook to 70°C). Grilled vegetables are excellent at room temperature, so this plate works for meal prep.
