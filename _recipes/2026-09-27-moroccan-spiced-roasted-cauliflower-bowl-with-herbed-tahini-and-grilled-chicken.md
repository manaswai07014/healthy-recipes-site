# Moroccan Spiced Roasted Cauliflower Bowl with Herbed Tahini and Grilled Chicken

Caramelized cauliflower florets and crispy chickpeas sing with warm Moroccan spices, finished with a cool, garlicky tahini drizzle and juicy grilled chicken breast.

## Ingredients

(Makes 3 servings)

- 150g chicken breast, trimmed
- 600g cauliflower florets
- 240g canned chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp sweet paprika
- ½ tsp ground cinnamon
- ½ tsp ground ginger
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 30g tahini
- 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 3 tbsp water
- 30g fresh parsley, chopped
- ½ tsp sea salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper

## Method

1. Step 1: Preheat oven to 220°C (425°F). Pat chicken breast dry with paper towels, season with ¼ tsp salt and ¼ tsp pepper. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in an oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat. Sear chicken 3-4 minutes until golden crust forms, then transfer skillet to oven.
2. Step 2: While chicken roasts, toss cauliflower florets and drained chickpeas with remaining 2 tbsp olive oil, cumin, coriander, paprika, cinnamon, ginger, and remaining salt and pepper in a large bowl. Spread on a baking sheet in a single layer.
3. Step 3: Roast vegetables for 25-28 minutes, stirring halfway, until cauliflower edges caramelize to deep golden brown and chickpeas are crispy. The internal temperature of chicken should reach 75°C (165°F).
4. Step 4: Meanwhile, whisk tahini, lemon juice, minced garlic, and water in a small bowl until smooth and pourable. The sauce should be the consistency of heavy cream—add more water if needed.
5. Step 5: Remove chicken from oven, let rest 3 minutes, then slice against the grain into ½-inch strips. Arrange cauliflower and chickpea mixture on plates, top with chicken slices, drizzle generously with tahini sauce, and garnish with fresh parsley. Serve immediately.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 485 kcal
- Protein: 32 g
- Carbohydrates: 38 g
- Fat: 26 g
- Fiber: 9 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For extra crunch, crush some chickpeas before roasting. The tahini sauce can be made ahead—store refrigerated up to 3 days, thin with water before using. Swap chicken for roasted salmon (cook to 63°C) or keep it vegetarian by doubling the chickpeas. Leftovers reheat well in a 180°C oven for 10 minutes—skip the tahini until serving to maintain its bright color.
