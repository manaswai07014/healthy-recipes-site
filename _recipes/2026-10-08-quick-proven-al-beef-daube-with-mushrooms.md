# Quick Provençal Beef Daube with Mushrooms

A streamlined Provençal daube leveraging quick-tenderizing techniques: sear beef in a hot Dutch oven until deeply caramelized, then braise with mushrooms, tomato, and a splash of red wine until fork-tender. The sauce reducción creates layers of savory depth without excess fat.

## Ingredients

(Makes 3 servings)

- 170g lean beef sirloin, sliced against the grain
- 200g cremini mushrooms, quartered
- 1 yellow onion, thinly sliced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 80ml dry red wine
- 360ml low-sodium beef broth
- 1.5 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- ½ tsp dried rosemary
- ½ tsp dried oregano
- 1 bay leaf
- Freshly ground black pepper
- Pinch of flaky sea salt

## Method

1. Step 1: Pat beef dry with paper towels, then slice against the grain into thin strips about ½cm thick. Season lightly with salt and pepper. The dry surface ensures maximum browning during searing.
2. Step 2: Heat a Dutch oven over high heat until smoking. Add 1 tbsp olive oil, swirl to coat. Sear beef in a single layer—don't crowd the pan. Cook 45-60 seconds per side until deeply browned but still slightly rare inside. Remove to a plate; tent with foil.
3. Step 3: Reduce heat to medium. Add remaining ½ tbsp oil to the same pot. Sauté onions with a pinch of salt, stirring occasionally, until softened and golden at the edges—about 5 minutes. Add garlic, thyme, rosemary, oregano; cook until fragrant, 30 seconds.
4. Step 4: Push onions to one side. Add tomato paste directly to the empty center; cook, stirring constantly, until it darkens to a deep brick-red color, about 2 minutes. This caramelization adds savory depth.
5. Step 5: Pour in red wine, scraping up any browned bits (fond). Simmer until reduced by half, about 2 minutes. Add beef broth and bay leaf; bring to a gentle simmer.
6. Step 6: Return beef and any accumulated juices to the pot. Cover partially; simmer on medium-low for 12-15 minutes until beef is fork-tender and sauce has thickened slightly. Stir once halfway.
7. Step 7: Add mushrooms in the final 5 minutes. They should be tender but retain shape. Remove bay leaf. Adjust seasoning with salt and pepper. The sauce should coat a spoon—if too thin, uncover and simmer 2 more minutes.
8. Step 8: Serve immediately in shallow bowls. The beef should be meltingly tender, mushrooms earthy, sauce rich with wine-herb complexity.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 445 kcal
- Protein: 35.7 g
- Carbohydrates: 18 g
- Fat: 22 g
- Fiber: 4 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For deeper flavor, marinate beef in 1 tbsp red wine + 1 minced garlic clove for 30 mins before cooking. Swap cremini for wild mushroom blend for more earthiness. This daube improves overnight—refrigerate up to 3 days and reheat gently. Serve over cauliflower mash to keep calories low, or with a small portion of whole wheat crusty bread.
