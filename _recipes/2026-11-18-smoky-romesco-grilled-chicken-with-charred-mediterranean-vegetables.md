# Smoky Romesco Grilled Chicken with Charred Mediterranean Vegetables

Chicken sizzles beside peppers, zucchini, and onion until charred at the edges. A smoky almond romesco adds tangy heat and richness without a heavy sauce.

## Ingredients

(Makes 2 servings)

- 300 g boneless, skinless chicken breast
- 1 medium zucchini, cut into thick half-moons
- 1 red bell pepper, cut into broad strips
- 1 small red onion, cut into wedges
- 200 g roasted or canned tomatoes, drained
- 30 g blanched almonds
- 1 slice whole-grain bread, torn
- 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 tbsp sherry vinegar
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/4 tsp fine salt
- 1/4 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- 1/8 tsp cayenne pepper

## Method

1. Step 1: Pat the chicken dry and cut it into two 150 g portions. Season with half the salt and pepper. Heat a grill pan or large skillet over medium-high heat and lightly oil it with 1 teaspoon of olive oil. Sear the chicken for 5-6 minutes without moving it, until browned patches form. Turn and cook for another 5-7 minutes, until the thickest point reaches 75°C. Transfer to a plate and rest while the vegetables cook.
2. Step 2: Increase the pan heat to high. Toss the zucchini, bell pepper, and onion with the remaining olive oil and salt. Arrange them cut-side down in a single layer. Leave undisturbed for 3-4 minutes to blacken slightly, then turn and cook for 5-7 minutes more. The vegetables should be tender with softened edges and lightly charred, but still hold their shape.
3. Step 3: Put the almonds in a small dry skillet over medium heat. Toast for 2-3 minutes, shaking often, until they smell nutty and show golden patches. Remove them from the heat so they do not scorch.
4. Step 4: Blend the drained tomatoes, toasted almonds, torn bread, sherry vinegar, smoked paprika, garlic powder, cayenne, and remaining black pepper with 2 tablespoons of water. Process for 45-60 seconds until mostly smooth. For a thicker sauce, add water one tablespoon at a time; for a coarser texture, pulse only until the almonds are broken.
5. Step 5: Taste the romesco and adjust with salt or a splash of vinegar. Slice the rested chicken across the grain. Divide the grilled vegetables and chicken between two plates, then spoon the romesco over the chicken and vegetables. Serve the sauce warm or at room temperature.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 498 kcal
- Protein: 48 g
- Carbohydrates: 28 g
- Fat: 21 g
- Fiber: 8 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For a more traditional romesco profile, use roasted red peppers in place of half the tomatoes and add a spoonful of mild harissa. If the sauce separates after standing, whisk in a teaspoon of cold water. Refrigerate leftover romesco in an airtight container for up to three days, adding a splash of water before serving. Store cooked chicken separately and reheat until steaming hot and 75°C throughout.
