introduction: |
  This light and fragrant soup is a Mediterranean take on a classic comfort dish. White fish, potatoes, leeks and carrots cook in a herby broth finished with fresh dill and lemon. Each serving delivers 22g of protein for just 248 kcal — perfect for a satisfying weeknight dinner that won't weigh you down.
instructions:
  - "Heat the oil in a large saucepan over medium heat. Add the onion and cook for 5-7 minutes until softening. Stir in the garlic, potatoes, carrots, peppers, leeks and tomato purée; cook for 3-5 minutes more."
  - "Pour in the vegetable stock, add the bay leaf, and bring to a simmer. Cover and cook for 15 minutes until the vegetables are almost tender."
  - "Finely chop most of the dill, reserving a few sprigs for garnish. Cut the fish into bite-sized pieces and season with salt, pepper and a little lemon juice."
  - "Add the fish to the soup with a pinch of lemon zest and the chopped dill. Simmer gently over low heat for 5-7 minutes until the fish is just cooked through."
  - "Remove the bay leaf, taste and adjust seasoning with more lemon juice or zest as needed. Ladle into bowls and garnish with the reserved dill sprigs."
chef_tips: |
  For an even lighter version, swap the potato for 200g of cauliflower florets — saves about 30 kcal per portion. The soup keeps well in the fridge for up to 2 days; the lemon flavour actually deepens on the second day. If you can't find hake, cod, haddock or pollack all work beautifully. Avoid oily fish like salmon or mackerel, which will overpower the delicate broth.
