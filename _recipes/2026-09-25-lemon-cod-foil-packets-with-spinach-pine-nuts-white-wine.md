# Lemon-Cod Foil Packets with Spinach, Pine Nuts & White Wine

Tender cod fillets steam inside parchment or foil with garlicky spinach, toasted pine nuts, and bright lemon — the packets trap aromatic steam that gently flakes the fish while infusing it with Mediterranean brightness.

## Ingredients

(Makes 2 servings)

- 2 cod fillets (170g each)
- 200g baby spinach
- 40g pine nuts
- 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 lemon, zested and juiced
- 60ml dry white wine
- 4g fresh dill, chopped
- Sea salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Cut two 30cm sheets of parchment paper or aluminum foil, folding each in half to create a crease.
2. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add pine nuts and toast for 2-3 minutes, stirring frequently, until golden with a nutty aroma. Transfer to a small bowl and set aside.
3. In the same skillet, add remaining olive oil and garlic. Sauté for 30 seconds until fragrant but not browned — watch for the garlic to turn translucent and release its sweet aroma.
4. Add spinach in batches, tossing with tongs until just wilted. This takes about 2-3 minutes total. The spinach should be bright green and tender but not waterlogged. Season lightly with salt and pepper.
5. Divide the spinach mixture between the two parchment sheets, spreading each into an even layer in the center.
6. Place one cod fillet on top of each spinach bed. Season generously with salt and pepper. Drizzle each fillet with half the lemon juice, then sprinkle with lemon zest and fresh dill.
7. Pour 30ml white wine over each packet, then gather the sides of the parchment and fold the edges tightly to create a sealed parcel. Leave a small gap at the top for steam to escape initially, then crimp shut.
8. Place packets on a baking sheet and bake for 12-15 minutes. The parcels should puff up with steam. Cod is done when it reaches 63°C (145°F) internally and flakes easily with a fork.
9. Carefully open the packets (steam will escape — avert your face). The cod should be opaque and the spinach silky. Garnish with toasted pine nuts and serve immediately with crusty bread or a light salad.

- Calories: 485 kcal
- Protein: 33 g
- Carbohydrates: 12 g
- Fat: 33 g
- Fiber: 4 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For extra depth, add a thin layer of cherry tomatoes or olives to the spinach before sealing. If pine nuts are unavailable, swap with sliced almonds or walnuts — toast them the same way. These packets can be assembled ahead and refrigerated for up to 4 hours before baking. Open the packet tableside for dramatic presentation, or transfer to shallow bowls and spoon the pan juices over the top.
