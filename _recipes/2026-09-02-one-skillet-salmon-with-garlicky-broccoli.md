instructions:
  - "Pat the salmon fillets dry with paper towels. Season generously with salt, pepper, and half the lemon zest."
  - "Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Place the salmon fillets skin-side down and cook for 4 minutes without moving, until the skin is crispy and golden."
  - "Flip the salmon, reduce heat to medium, and cook for another 2-3 minutes until just opaque. Transfer to a plate and tent with foil to keep warm."
  - "Add the remaining 2 tbsp olive oil to the same skillet. Add the broccoli florets, season with a pinch of salt, and sauté for 3-4 minutes until bright green and starting to char at the edges."
  - "Push the broccoli to one side of the skillet. Add the minced garlic and red pepper flakes to the cleared space and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant."
  - "Pour in the broth (or water) and the lemon juice. Scrape up any browned bits from the bottom of the pan. Let everything simmer for 1 minute to meld the flavors."
  - "Return the salmon to the skillet, spoon some of the garlicky broccoli mixture over the top, and sprinkle with fresh parsley and the remaining lemon zest."
  - "Serve immediately, straight from the skillet or plated with a wedge of lemon on the side."

chef_tips: |
  For the crispiest salmon skin, make sure to pat the fillets completely dry before seasoning and don't move them once they hit the hot pan. If your broccoli is on the larger side, cut the florets smaller so they cook through in the same time as the salmon. Save the salmon skin separately as a crispy garnish if you like — once it's shatteringly crisp, lift it off and break it into shards to sprinkle over the finished dish. Pairs beautifully with a side of quinoa or farro if you want to stretch it into a more substantial meal.
