# Sicilian Swordfish Rolls Stuffed with Pine Nuts, Raisins, and Herbs

Tender swordfish steaks are rolled around a sweet-savory stuffing of toasted pine nuts, plump raisins, and fresh herbs, then baked with white wine until perfectly flaky at 63°C. The contrast of textures—crispy breadcrumb exterior against buttery fish—delivers the rustic elegance of a Sicilian trattoria in under an hour.

## Ingredients

(Makes 3 servings)

- 400g center-cut swordfish steak
- 60g fresh breadcrumbs
- 35g pine nuts
- 45g golden raisins
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 15g fresh parsley, chopped
- 8g fresh basil, chopped
- 35ml extra virgin olive oil
- 100ml dry white wine
- Fine sea salt and black pepper, to taste

## Method

1. Toast the pine nuts in a dry skillet over medium heat, stirring constantly, until golden and fragrant—about 3 minutes. Watch closely; they burn quickly. Transfer to a bowl immediately.
2. Combine breadcrumbs, toasted pine nuts, raisins, garlic, parsley, and basil in a mixing bowl. Drizzle with 20ml olive oil and toss until evenly moistened. The mixture should hold together when pressed. Season with salt and pepper.
3. Cut the swordfish steak into 3 equal portions, roughly 130g each. Place each piece between plastic wrap and gently pound to 1cm thickness for even rolling.
4. Spoon 2 tablespoons of the stuffing onto each swordfish portion. Roll tightly and secure with a toothpick. The roll should be snug but not compressed.
5. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Heat remaining 15ml olive oil in an oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat. Sear the rolls seam-side down for 2 minutes until golden—resist moving them to achieve a proper crust.
6. Flip rolls and pour white wine around them. Transfer skillet to oven and bake 12-15 minutes until internal temperature reaches 63°C (145°F) and fish flakes easily. The wine will reduce to a fragrant pan sauce.
7. Rest rolls 2 minutes in the pan sauce. Remove toothpicks and serve immediately, spooning the reduced wine sauce over the top.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 485 kcal
- Protein: 31 g
- Carbohydrates: 38 g
- Fat: 22 g
- Fiber: 2 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For gluten-free version, substitute breadcrumbs with crushed almond meal. The raisins can be soaked in warm water for 10 minutes if you prefer softer texture in the filling. If swordfish steaks aren't available, halibut or thick cod fillets work as alternatives—adjust cooking time slightly. Store leftovers refrigerated for up to 2 days; reheat gently in a 150°C oven to maintain texture.
