# Mediterranean Berry Protein Bowl with Honey-Toasted Granola

A protein-forward breakfast bowl featuring velvety Greek yogurt layered with jewel-toned berries, a buttery honey-almond granola, and a sprinkling of omega-rich chia seeds.

## Ingredients

(Makes 2 servings)

- 150g nonfat Greek yogurt per bowl
- 80g mixed berries (blueberries, raspberries, blackberries)
- 50g homemade honey-almond granola
- 20g sliced almonds
- 10g chia seeds
- Drizzle of honey
- Lemon zest and vanilla for brightness

## Method

1. Step 1: Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. Combine oats, sliced almonds, a pinch of sea salt, and lemon zest in a bowl.
2. Step 2: Drizzle olive oil and honey over the oat mixture. Toss thoroughly to coat each oat flake. Spread in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet.
3. Step 3: Bake for 12-15 minutes, stirring halfway, until the granola turns golden and the almonds emit a fragrant, nutty aroma. Remove and let cool completely—it will crisp as it cools.
4. Step 4: While granola cools, portion Greek yogurt into two bowls. Smooth the surface with the back of a spoon for an even base.
5. Step 5: Arrange mixed berries in a neat cluster over the yogurt. The colors should contrast—deep blue blueberries, ruby raspberries, and inky blackberries.
6. Step 6: Sprinkle 25g of the cooled granola over each bowl. Add chia seeds in a small mound for texture and visual interest.
7. Step 7: Finish with a light drizzle of honey and an extra scatter of almonds. Serve immediately.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 412 kcal
- Protein: 22 g
- Carbohydrates: 48 g
- Fat: 16 g
- Fiber: 6 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For meal prep, store granola in an airtight container up to 1 week. Swap raspberries for strawberries in season, or add a tablespoon of almond butter for extra creaminess. The yogurt can be sweetened with a few drops of stevia if reducing added sugars.
