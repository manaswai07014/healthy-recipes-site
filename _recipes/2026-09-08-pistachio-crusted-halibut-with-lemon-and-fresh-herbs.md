# Pistachio-Crusted Halibut with Lemon and Fresh Herbs

A crisp pistachio crust meets buttery halibut in this elegant dish. The nuts are toasted until fragrant before crushing, creating a textural contrast that shatters beautifully against the flaky fish.

## Ingredients

(Makes 2 servings)

- 200g halibut fillet
- 40g raw pistachios
- 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 1 medium lemon
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, finely chopped
- 1 tbsp fresh dill, finely chopped
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 30g breadcrumbs
- 1 large egg white
- Sea salt and freshly ground black pepper

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper and lightly oil with 1/2 tbsp olive oil.
2. Toast pistachios on the baking sheet for 4-5 minutes until fragrant and deepened in color. Watch carefully—they burn quickly. Remove and let cool for 3 minutes.
3. Crush pistachios roughly using a mortar and pestle or pulse in a food processor twice—they should be coarse crumbs, not a paste.
4. In a bowl, combine crushed pistachios, breadcrumbs, minced garlic, parsley, dill, remaining 1/2 tbsp olive oil, lemon zest, salt, and pepper. Mix well.
5. Pat halibut dry with paper towels. Brush one side with egg white (this helps the crust adhere). Press the pistachio mixture firmly onto the fish, coating the top evenly.
6. Heat an oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat. Add 1/2 tbsp olive oil. Sear halibut crust-side down for 2 minutes until crust is golden and fragrant—you'll hear a sizzle. Don't move it.
7. Flip fish carefully. Transfer skillet to oven and bake for 8-10 minutes until fish reaches 63°C (145°F) internal temperature and flakes easily with a fork.
8. While fish rests for 2 minutes, make the lemon herb sauce: squeeze 2 tbsp lemon juice into a small bowl, add 1 tsp olive oil, pinch of salt, and a sprinkle of fresh herbs.
9. Plate halibut on warmed plates, drizzle with lemon sauce, and garnish with lemon wedges and extra herb sprigs.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 420 kcal
- Protein: 32 g
- Carbohydrates: 18 g
- Fat: 24 g
- Fiber: 3 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For extra crunch, add 1 tbsp panko breadcrumbs to the crust mixture. Substitute salmon or cod if halibut is unavailable—adjust cooking time by 2 minutes. The crust can be prepped ahead and refrigerated for up to 4 hours. Store leftover crust in an airtight container for up to 1 week. To make dairy-free, skip any butter-based sauces and use olive oil throughout.
