# Greek Lamb Burgers with Herb-Feta Yogurt Sauce

Juicy lamb burgers seasoned with oregano and cumin, topped with a cooling feta-yogurt sauce infused with fresh dill and lemon zest.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 500g ground lamb
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1/4 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/4 tsp fine sea salt
- 1/4 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- 150g Greek yogurt (2% fat)
- 80g feta cheese, crumbled
- 1 small cucumber, finely diced
- 2 tbsp fresh dill, chopped
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 tsp lemon zest
- 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 2 whole wheat burger buns
- 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 4 butter lettuce leaves
- 1 medium tomato, sliced
- 1/4 red onion, thinly sliced

## Method

1. Step 1: Combine ground lamb with oregano, cumin, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper in a large bowl. Mix gently with clean hands until just combined—overworking creates tough burgers.
2. Step 2: Divide mixture into 4 equal portions (about 125g each) and form into patties slightly wider than the buns, about 1cm thick. Press a shallow indent in the center of each patty to prevent bulging during cooking. Set aside.
3. Step 3: For the sauce, combine Greek yogurt, crumbled feta, diced cucumber, dill, minced garlic, lemon zest, and lemon juice in a small bowl. Mash feta slightly with a fork to create a textured sauce. Season with a pinch of salt and pepper. Refrigerate until needed.
4. Step 4: Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Add lamb patties and cook for 4-5 minutes per side for medium doneness—internal temperature should reach 70°C. The exterior should develop a deep golden-brown crust.
5. Step 5: While patties rest for 2 minutes, toast the burger buns cut-side down in the same skillet until golden, about 30 seconds.
6. Step 6: Assemble burgers: layer lettuce on the bottom bun, add the lamb patty, top with tomato slices and red onion rings, then generously spoon the herb-feta yogurt sauce over the top. Close with the bun top.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 485 kcal
- Protein: 25 g
- Carbohydrates: 28 g
- Fat: 29 g
- Fiber: 4 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For extra flavor depth, add 1/4 tsp cinnamon to the lamb seasoning. The sauce can be made ahead and stored refrigerated for up to 3 days—texture thickens as it sits. Swap ground lamb for lean ground turkey to reduce calories further, though the traditional lamb delivers authentic Greek character. If feta is too salty, rinse briefly before crumbling.
