# Greek Lamb Burgers with Herb-Feta Yogurt Sauce

Mediterranean lamb burgers with warm cumin and oregano, topped with a cooling feta-yogurt sauce brightened with fresh dill and mint.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 500g ground lamb
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- ½ tsp fine sea salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- 150g non-fat Greek yogurt
- 60g reduced-fat feta cheese
- 2 tbsp fresh dill, chopped
- 1 tbsp fresh mint, chopped
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- 4 whole wheat burger buns
- ¼ red onion, thinly sliced
- 1 tomato, sliced
- ½ cucumber, sliced
- 4 butter lettuce leaves

## Method

1. Step 1: Combine Greek yogurt, crumbled feta, chopped dill, mint, lemon juice, and a pinch of salt in a small bowl. Mash feta with a fork until partially incorporated. Sauce should be thick but spreadable. Set aside.
2. Step 2: In a large bowl, combine ground lamb, cumin, oregano, minced garlic, salt, and pepper. Mix gently with hands until just combined—overworking creates tough burgers.
3. Step 3: Divide mixture into 4 equal portions (about 125g each). Form into patties slightly larger than buns, about 1cm thick. Press center slightly thinner to ensure even cooking.
4. Step 4: Heat a cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. No oil needed—lamb has sufficient fat. Cook patties 4-5 minutes per side for medium, achieving deep brown crust with visible grill marks.
5. Step 5: During final minute of cooking, toast buns cut-side down in the same skillet for 60-90 seconds until golden and crisp.
6. Step 6: Internal temperature should reach 74°C+ for lamb. Rest patties 2 minutes on a plate— juices redistribute for a moist burger.
7. Step 7: Build burgers: bottom bun, lettuce leaf, lamb patty, generous tablespoons of feta-yogurt sauce, tomato slices, cucumber, red onion, top bun.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 545 kcal
- Protein: 31 g
- Carbohydrates: 34 g
- Fat: 32 g
- Fiber: 5 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For extra Mediterranean flair, add 2 tbsp chopped kalamata olives to the lamb mixture. Swap beef for lamb if preferred—same method works. Sauce keeps refrigerated up to 3 days; texture tightens overnight, stir in a splash of water before serving. If feta is too salty, rinse briefly and pat dry before using.
