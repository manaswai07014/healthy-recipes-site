# Mediterranean Shrimp Pasta with White Wine

Seared shrimp develop a golden crust as you deglaze the pan with crisp white wine, creating a sauce that clings to whole wheat linguine. Finish with a bright squeeze of lemon and fresh parsley.

## Ingredients

(Makes 2 servings)

- 120g whole wheat linguine
- 200g raw jumbo shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 200g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 medium zucchini, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 120ml dry white wine
- 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
- 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
- Salt and black pepper to taste

## Method

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to boil. Add linguine and cook according to package directions until al dente, about 10-12 minutes. Reserve 1/2 cup pasta water before draining.
2. While pasta cooks, pat shrimp completely dry with paper towels. Season generously with salt and pepper. This ensures proper browning.
3. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Add shrimp in a single layer—listen for a lively sizzle. Cook 2 minutes without moving until golden on bottom, then flip and cook 1 minute more. Shrimp should be opaque and reach 63°C internally. Remove to a plate.
4. Reduce heat to medium. Add diced zucchini to the same pan. Saute 3-4 minutes until tender but still holding shape, stirring occasionally. Add a pinch of salt.
5. Add minced garlic and red pepper flakes. Stir for 30 seconds until fragrant—watch carefully to prevent burning. The aroma should be potent and slightly sweet.
6. Pour in white wine, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom. Let simmer 2-3 minutes until reduced by half. The liquid should coat a spoon slightly.
7. Add cherry tomatoes and cook 2 minutes until they begin to soften and release their juices. The sauce will loosen and become glossy.
8. Return shrimp to the pan along with any accumulated juices. Add half the lemon juice and toss to combine. If sauce seems thick, add reserved pasta water one tablespoon at a time.
9. Add drained pasta directly to the sauce. Toss vigorously for 1-2 minutes so pasta absorbs the flavor and sauce clings to each strand. The sauce should emulsify slightly.
10. Remove from heat. Add remaining lemon juice and fresh parsley. Toss once more. Taste and adjust seasoning—add more pepper flakes if desired.
11. Divide between two warm bowls. Serve immediately with lemon wedges on the side.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 485 kcal
- Protein: 38 g
- Carbohydrates: 52 g
- Fat: 14 g
- Fiber: 8 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For extra protein, add 100g cooked mussels in the last 2 minutes. Whole wheat pasta adds fiber (6g per serving) while keeping calories reasonable. Substitute gluten-free pasta if needed—the sauce works identically. Leftovers keep refrigerated for 2 days; add a splash of water when reheating to loosen the sauce. The shrimp texture actually improves next day as flavors meld.
