# Greek Lemon Chicken Souvlaki with Tzatziki and Warm Pita

Tender marinated chicken breast threaded onto skewers, grilled until charred at the edges, served with thick cucumber tzatziki and warm pita—a street-food classic reimagined for home kitchens.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 400g skinless chicken breast, cubed
- 3 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp sea salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 200g non-fat Greek yogurt
- 1 medium cucumber, grated and drained
- 2 tbsp fresh dill, chopped
- 4 whole wheat pita breads
- 1 medium red onion, sliced
- 2 medium tomatoes, chopped

## Method

1. Step 1: Combine lemon juice, 1 tbsp olive oil, minced garlic, oregano, salt, and pepper in a large bowl. Add chicken cubes and toss to coat. Cover and marinate for 15 minutes at room temperature or up to 2 hours refrigerated.
2. Step 2: Meanwhile, prepare the tzatziki: grate cucumber onto a clean kitchen towel, squeeze out excess moisture, then mix with Greek yogurt, remaining 1 tbsp olive oil, dill, and a pinch of salt. Refrigerate until serving.
3. Step 3: Thread marinated chicken onto 4 metal or wooden skewers (about 100g per skewer). Heat a grill pan or cast-iron skillet over high heat until smoking.
4. Step 4: Grill skewers for 3-4 minutes per side, turning once, until internal temperature reaches 75°C (165°F) and exterior shows golden char marks. Listen for the sizzle and aromatics—garlic and oregano should perfume the air.
5. Step 5: While chicken rests for 2 minutes, warm pita breads in the same pan for 30 seconds per side until pliable and lightly toasted. Alternatively, wrap in foil and heat in oven at 180°C for 3 minutes.
6. Step 6: Assemble: slice chicken from skewers or serve whole. Arrange on a platter with warm pita, sliced red onion, chopped tomatoes, and a generous dollop of tzatziki. Drizzle with extra lemon if desired.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 485 kcal
- Protein: 38 g
- Carbohydrates: 42 g
- Fat: 16 g
- Fiber: 6 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For extra smoky flavor, add a pinch of smoked paprika to the chicken marinade. Swap chicken for pork tenderloin if preferred—cook to 70°C internal. Make tzatziki ahead; flavors meld beautifully overnight. Leftover tzatziki keeps 3 days refrigerated and makes an excellent dip for raw vegetables. To grill outdoors, use medium-high heat and turn skewers every 2 minutes for even cooking.
