# Quick Chicken Osso Buco with Lemon Gremolata and Creamy Polenta

Searing chicken thighs until golden, then braising in tomato-wine sauce builds deep flavor. Gremolata's lemon zest and parsley brighten. 465 kcal, 39g protein.

## Ingredients

(Makes 4 servings)

- 680 g boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 medium yellow onion, finely chopped
- 1 medium carrot, finely chopped
- 1 celery stalk, finely chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, divided (2 minced for braise, 1 minced for gremolata)
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1/2 cup dry white wine
- 1 can (400 g) diced tomatoes, no salt added
- 1 cup low-sodium chicken broth
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 bay leaf
- 1/2 tsp salt, plus more for polenta
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1/4 cup fresh parsley, finely chopped
- Zest of 1 lemon
- 1 cup dry polenta
- 4 cups water

## Method

1. Step 1: Pat the chicken thighs dry with paper towels. Season both sides with 1/2 tsp salt and 1/4 tsp black pepper. Finely chop the onion, carrot, and celery. Mince 2 garlic cloves for the braise and set aside 1 clove for the gremolata. Zest the lemon and chop the parsley for the gremolata; combine in a small bowl and refrigerate until serving.
2. Step 2: Heat olive oil in a Dutch oven or heavy pot over medium-high heat. When the oil shimmers, add the chicken thighs in a single layer. Sear for 4–5 minutes per side until deeply golden brown. You should hear a steady sizzle. Transfer the chicken to a plate; it will not be cooked through yet.
3. Step 3: Reduce heat to medium. Add the onion, carrot, and celery to the pot. Cook for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until softened and translucent. Scrape up the browned bits (fond) from the bottom. Add the 2 minced garlic cloves and tomato paste; cook for 1 minute until fragrant and the paste darkens slightly.
4. Step 4: Pour in the white wine and simmer for 2 minutes, scraping the bottom of the pot to dissolve the fond. Add the diced tomatoes, chicken broth, dried oregano, and bay leaf. Return the chicken and any accumulated juices to the pot. Bring to a gentle simmer, cover, and reduce heat to low. Cook for 20–25 minutes, or until the chicken reaches an internal temperature of 75°C (167°F) and the sauce has thickened slightly.
5. Step 5: While the chicken braises, make the polenta. In a medium saucepan, bring 4 cups water to a boil. Add a pinch of salt. Slowly whisk in the dry polenta. Reduce heat to low and cook, stirring frequently, for 15–20 minutes until thick and creamy. If using instant polenta, follow package timing. Keep warm.
6. Step 6: Remove the bay leaf from the braise. Taste the sauce and adjust salt if needed. To serve, spoon creamy polenta into shallow bowls. Top with chicken thighs and generous spoonfuls of the tomato-wine sauce. Sprinkle the lemon-parsley gremolata over the top. Serve immediately.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 465 kcal
- Protein: 39 g
- Carbohydrates: 44 g
- Fat: 13 g
- Fiber: 5 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For a lower-carb side, swap the polenta for cauliflower purée—steam 1 head of cauliflower, then blend with a splash of broth and a pinch of salt. The braise itself is forgiving: if you have leftover sauce, it freezes well for up to 3 months. Store leftovers in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days; reheat gently on the stovetop with a splash of broth to loosen the sauce. Gremolata is best made fresh, but you can chop the parsley and zest the lemon up to 4 hours ahead and keep covered in the fridge. If you avoid wine, replace it with 1/2 cup extra chicken broth plus 1 tablespoon lemon juice.
