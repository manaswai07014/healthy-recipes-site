# Sheet-Pan Miso-Glazed Tofu with Sesame Broccoli

Crispy-edged tofu caramelizes under a glossy miso glaze while broccoli florets toast until tender-crisp with a sesame crunch. The contrast of textures—silky interior against golden exterior—delivers depth without excess oil.

## Ingredients

(Makes 2 servings)

- 400g extra-firm tofu, pressed and cubed
- 400g broccoli florets
- 3 tbsp white miso paste
- 2 tbsp mirin
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp sesame seeds
- 1 green onion, sliced

## Method

1. Step 1: Press tofu between paper towels for 10 minutes to extract excess moisture—this creates a firmer texture that holds together during roasting and develops deeper browning.
2. Step 2: While tofu presses, whisk together miso paste, mirin, rice vinegar, sesame oil, grated ginger, and minced garlic in a bowl until smooth. The glaze should coat a spoon; add 1-2 tbsp water if too thick.
3. Step 3: Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a sheet pan with parchment paper. Toss broccoli florets with 1 tbsp vegetable oil, spread in a single layer on one side of the pan.
4. Step 4: Cube pressed tofu into 1-inch pieces. Gently toss tofu with remaining 1 tbsp vegetable oil, then coat thoroughly with half the miso glaze. Reserve remaining glaze for finishing.
5. Step 5: Arrange tofu on the opposite side of the pan in a single layer—crowding causes steaming rather than roasting. Roast for 12 minutes at 400°F.
6. Step 6: Remove pan, carefully flip tofu pieces using a thin spatula. Toss broccoli gently. Return to oven for 10-12 minutes until tofu edges caramelize and broccoli shows charred spots.
7. Step 7: Brush remaining glaze over hot tofu for a glossy finish. Sprinkle sesame seeds and green onions immediately—the residual heat toasts the seeds slightly. Serve immediately while the glaze remains sticky.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 520 kcal
- Protein: 30 g
- Carbohydrates: 38 g
- Fat: 28 g
- Fiber: 9 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For extra caramelization, broil on high for the final 2 minutes but watch carefully to prevent burning. Swap broccoli for cauliflower or asparagus depending on season. Leftover tofu reheats well in a dry pan for lunch the next day—press again before reheating to restore crispness. The glaze keeps in an airtight container for 1 week.
