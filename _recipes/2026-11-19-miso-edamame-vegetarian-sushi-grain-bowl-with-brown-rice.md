# Miso-Edamame Vegetarian Sushi Grain Bowl with Brown Rice

Nutty brown rice, plump edamame, and crisp vegetables meet a glossy miso-sesame dressing in this balanced sushi-style bowl.

## Ingredients

(Makes 2 servings)

- 100 g dry brown rice
- 160 g shelled edamame
- 1 small cucumber
- 1 medium carrot
- 100 g cherry tomatoes
- 1 small avocado
- 1 tbsp white miso paste
- 1 tbsp reduced-sodium soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 tsp sesame seeds
- 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
- 0.5 red onion, thinly sliced
- 1 tsp canola oil
- 0.25 tsp ground black pepper

## Method

1. Step 1: Rinse the brown rice under cold water for 30 seconds, then drain. Combine with 220 ml water in a saucepan, bring to a boil, cover, and simmer on low for 20 minutes. Remove from the heat and let it stand covered for 10 minutes; fork through the grains to loosen them.
2. Step 2: Bring a second pot of lightly salted water to a boil. Add the edamame and cook for 4 minutes until bright green and tender with a slight bite. Drain and cool briefly.
3. Step 3: Peel the carrot into thin ribbons, then slice the cucumber into narrow half-moons. Halve the cherry tomatoes and thinly slice the red onion. Toss the carrot with the canola oil and black pepper.
4. Step 4: Whisk the miso, soy sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil, and ginger with 1 tbsp warm water until smooth. The dressing should taste balanced: salty from the miso, bright from the vinegar, and aromatic from the ginger.
5. Step 5: Divide the brown rice between two bowls. Arrange the edamame, carrot ribbons, cucumber, tomatoes, red onion, and sliced avocado in separate sections. Spoon over the miso-sesame dressing and finish with sesame seeds.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 485 kcal
- Protein: 16 g
- Carbohydrates: 62 g
- Fat: 19 g
- Fiber: 13 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For a more substantial bowl, add 100 g firm tofu, pan-seared in a nonstick skillet until golden on each side, or use 100 g cooked quinoa in place of half the brown rice. Keep the dressing refrigerated for up to 3 days and add it only when serving so the rice does not become soggy.
