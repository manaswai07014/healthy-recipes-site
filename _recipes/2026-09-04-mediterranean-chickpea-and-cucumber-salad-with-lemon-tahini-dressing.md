# Mediterranean Chickpea and Cucumber Salad with Lemon-Tahini Dressing

A vibrant Mediterranean chickpea salad featuring crisp cucumber, juicy cherry tomatoes, and a silky lemon-tahini dressing. The tahini is whisked with lemon and garlic until emulsified, creating a luscious coating for the fiber-rich legumes.

## Ingredients

(Makes 3 servings)

- 400g canned chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 2 medium cucumbers, diced into 1cm cubes
- 150g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 80g red onion, thinly sliced
- 30g fresh parsley, roughly chopped
- 15g fresh mint leaves
- 30g tahini
- 1 large lemon, juiced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 tbsp cold water
- 1/2 tsp sea salt
- 1/4 tsp freshly ground black pepper
- 1/4 tsp smoked paprika, for garnish

## Method

1. Step 1: Prepare the vegetables. Dice cucumbers into uniform 1cm cubes for even texture—listen for the crisp snap as your knife cuts through. Halve cherry tomatoes and thinly slice the red onion into half-moons, about 3mm thick.
2. Step 2: Drain and rinse the chickpeas thoroughly. Pat them dry with a clean kitchen towel to remove excess moisture—this helps the dressing cling better. The chickpeas should feel slightly tacky but not wet.
3. Step 3: Make the tahini dressing. In a medium bowl, combine tahini, lemon juice, minced garlic, salt, and pepper. Whisk vigorously while gradually adding cold water one tablespoon at a time. The mixture will transform from thick and paste-like to a silky, pourable consistency with a pale ivory color. Stop adding water when the dressing coats a spoon without dripping immediately—this is your emulsification point.
4. Step 4: Assemble the salad. Add chickpeas to a large mixing bowl, followed by cucumbers, tomatoes, red onion, parsley, and mint. Drizzle the tahini dressing over the top.
5. Step 5: Toss gently using clean hands or a large spoon, ensuring every chickpea and vegetable piece is lightly coated. The dressing should cascade like thick silk between the ingredients. Taste and adjust seasoning—add more lemon for brightness or a pinch of salt for depth.
6. Step 6: Plate and garnish. Divide among bowls or spread on a platter. Finish with a light dusting of smoked paprika and an extra drizzle of tahini dressing if desired. Serve immediately for maximum crunch.

## Nutrition (per serving)

- Calories: 425 kcal
- Protein: 16 g
- Carbohydrates: 52 g
- Fat: 18 g
- Fiber: 12 g

## 👨‍🍳 Chef Tips

For extra protein, add 100g roasted edamame or extra chickpeas to reach 18g protein per serving. The dressing can be made ahead—store in an airtight container for up to 5 days; whisk well before using as it may thicken. Red onion can be soaked in ice water for 10 minutes if you prefer a milder, more mellow flavor. This salad holds well for lunch the next day; the cucumbers release their water, creating a more marinated texture. For a nut-free version, this recipe is already nut-free as written.
