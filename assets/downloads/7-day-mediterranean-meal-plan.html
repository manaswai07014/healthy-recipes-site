7-Day Mediterranean Meal Plan

Welcome! This 7-day plan delivers hand-crafted Mediterranean meals built around these principles:

  • ✓ 400-600 calories per meal
  • ✓ ≥15g protein per meal
  • ✓ Real, whole-food ingredients (olive oil, fish, vegetables, herbs, legumes)
  • ✓ Mediterranean diet fundamentals (lean protein + healthy fats + fiber)

Daily totals: ~1,600-1,800 kcal | ~95-110g protein | ~180-220g carbs

Cooking tips: Sunday meal prep: cook all beans, grains, and proteins for the week. Pantry essentials: extra virgin olive oil, lemons, garlic, herbs, sea salt.
