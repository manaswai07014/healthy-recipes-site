Why Low-Calorie Western Cooking?

Mediterranean and Western diets have centuries of tradition — and a treasure trove of dishes that happen to be naturally low in calories but rich in protein, fiber, and flavor. We focus on 400-600 kcal per serving with at least 15g of protein, so you can enjoy satisfying meals that fit your goals.

Every recipe on this site has been tested in our editorial kitchen and reviewed for nutritional accuracy. We don't just rephrase other sites — we cook the dish, photograph it, and tell you exactly what works.
